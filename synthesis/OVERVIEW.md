# Overview: what moves the SOL perpetual over minutes to hours, and how to build around it

Written 2026-11-22 from the 31 topic syntheses and the 530 source notes under `topics/`. This is a map for someone starting work on the bot: a directional trader of crypto perpetual futures on horizons of minutes to hours, with the SOL perpetual on Kalshi as the reference instrument.

How to read it:

- Every factual statement comes from a note or a topic synthesis and is linked. Where a sentence is this document's own reading across topics, it says "reading" or "inference".
- Evidence grades (A to D) and access levels follow [CONVENTIONS.md](../CONVENTIONS.md). Across the 530 notes the grades are 116 A, 252 B, 130 C and 32 D; 50 notes were written from an abstract only and 6 from a secondary description. Treat those 56 as pointers.
- Link targets are written relative to this file's folder, so they start with `../topics/`. The repository-relative path is the same string without the leading `../`.
- Three limits apply to almost everything below. SOL is rarely in the samples. Nothing has been measured on Kalshi beyond its documentation and a one-day data snapshot. Most reported effects are gross of costs.

## The short version

1. **The Kalshi SOL perp is a price taker.** Price discovery sits in the largest offshore perpetual, and smaller, newer or access-restricted venues follow within about a second ([topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)). Signals are computed on Binance and the other large venues; Kalshi is where the trade is executed.
2. **Costs set the playing field.** A retail account at the entry tier pays 12 bp to take and 2 bp to make, on a 4.5 bp median spread, against one-minute volatility near 8 bp ([fee schedule](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-perpetuals-fee-schedule-amendment.md), [snapshot](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-solperp-public-market-data-snapshot.md)). Most gross intraday edges reported in the notes are smaller than one taker round trip.
3. **The well-supported facts are about risk, not direction.** Volatility, volume and liquidity are forecastable and strongly periodic; regimes are volatility regimes; liquidity measures predict volatility better than they predict sign ([topic 14](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 10](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 01](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 09](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/SYNTHESIS.md)).
4. **Direction is conditional.** Whether a move continues or reverts depends on why the flow happened (forced or informed), how fast the move was, how liquid the market was and which horizon is asked about ([topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 08](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md)). No unconditional rule survives.
5. **Slow variables condition; they do not predict.** Macro state, funding, open interest, positioning and the clock change how large a response is and how risky it is to hold. None has demonstrated out-of-sample directional content at minutes to hours ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 03](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 04](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 18](../topics/18-positioning-sentiment/SYNTHESIS.md)).
6. **Simple models, equal weights, strict accounting.** Shallow models match deep ones, averaging beats selecting, and the best of many backtests is biased upward by a computable amount ([topic 23](../topics/23-ml-feature-prediction/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 29](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md)).
7. **Reinforcement learning and evolutionary search contribute method more than edge.** The notes support borrowing RL's problem formulation and using RL for execution, and using evolutionary search inside tight controls. They do not support an end-to-end learned trader on intraday crypto ([topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)).
8. **Popular chart and positioning lore is mostly untested or fails controlled tests.** Liquidation heatmaps, smart-money concepts, long/short ratios, cycle indicators and liquidity-lead claims are graded D or have evidence against them ([topic 17](../topics/17-auction-orderflow-practitioner/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 18](../topics/18-positioning-sentiment/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 12](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/SYNTHESIS.md)).

## 1. The causal picture: what moves the SOL perp price

Read the Kalshi SOL perp price as the last link in a chain. Slow layers set the state; fast layers produce the move. Each layer below is ordered from slowest to fastest, with what the notes establish and how the layer conditions the next one.

| Layer | Timescale | What it sets | Evidence for the layer as a state variable | Evidence for it as a directional predictor at minutes to hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 Macro regime and risk appetite | days to weeks | Crypto's beta to equities, sensitivity to policy news, volatility level | B, daily and weekly | Weak; lagged macro predictors mostly fail out of sample |
| 1.2 Scheduled macro news | minutes to hours, known dates | Volatility and spread state; sign depends on regime | A for the method, B for crypto after 2021 | B but unstable across samples |
| 1.3 Crypto market factor and cross-asset links | minutes to days | Most of SOL's variance; the residual is what is SOL-specific | A and B | Lead of BTC over liquid coins is at most about a minute |
| 1.4 Leverage, funding and positioning | hours to days | How far a shock travels; whether flow is forced | B for mechanism | Not shown; the multi-event tests found are negative |
| 1.5 SOL-specific flows and options | minutes to days | Risk flags and volatility inputs | C and D for SOL | Untested on SOL |
| 1.6 The clock | deterministic | Volatility, volume, spread and toxicity by time of day, week and funding cycle | A | Weak and unstable |
| 1.7 Volatility and liquidity state | minutes to hours | Size of move per unit of flow; trading cost; reward for fading | A and B | Predicts volatility, not sign |
| 1.8 Trade flow and shocks | minutes to hours | The signed move and its transitory share | A in equities and FX, B in crypto | Yes, conditionally, and small next to costs |
| 1.9 Order book and venue transmission | milliseconds to seconds | The last few ticks; who quotes first | A | Yes for seconds; not capturable by a taker |

### 1.1 Macro regime and risk appetite (days to weeks)

- The macro link is a post-2020 feature. The weekly correlation of the coin market with global equities is 1% before 2020 and 39% after ([Borri et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/borri-2026-cryptocurrency-investable-asset-class.md)). Bitcoin's beta to US equities on 30-minute returns in US hours was near zero before 2020 and persistently positive after a break around late 2020, and the exposure is to the broad market, with the NASDAQ adding little once the Dow is controlled for ([Drexler et al. 2026](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/drexler-2026-bitcoin-rising-beta-equities.md)).
- US monetary policy drives a single global factor in risky assets ([Miranda-Agrippino and Rey 2019](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/miranda-agrippino-2019-us-monetary-policy-global-financial-cycle.md)). A single crypto factor explains 67% of the price variance of major coins and falls 0.41 standard deviations per percentage point of Fed surprise; the effect is concentrated when institutional participation and Bitcoin funding are both high, and comparable ECB and Bank of England surprises produce no negative response ([Copestake et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/copestake-2026-crypto-cycle-institutional-investors.md)).
- Regimes are mostly volatility regimes. Equal means across regimes cannot be rejected while equal volatilities are ([Ang and Timmermann 2011](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/notes/ang-2011-regime-changes-financial-markets.md)); the tested payoff of regime detection is lower volatility and drawdown, not better return prediction ([Bulla et al. 2010](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/notes/bulla-2010-markov-switching-asset-allocation-profitable.md), [Shu et al. 2024](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/notes/shu-2024-downside-risk-statistical-jump-model.md)).
- Lagged macro variables are poor directional predictors of crypto. In a 141-predictor audit of daily Bitcoin returns, 5.6% of macro exercises survive out of sample against 32.2% of technical ones ([Sanhueza 2026](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/sanhueza-2026-bitcoin-return-predictability-comprehensive.md)). A fixed M2 lead fails a sign-stability test ([Molnar 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/molnar-2026-bitcoin-halving-clock.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: the regime sets how strongly crypto co-moves with equities, how large and of which sign the response to scheduled news is, and the level of volatility. It supplies context, not a trade.

### 1.2 Scheduled macro news (minutes to hours, on known dates)

- The measurement template is settled: a standardised surprise, regressed on the window return using announcement observations only ([Andersen et al. 2002](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/andersen-2002-micro-effects-macro-announcements-fx.md)). In FX and futures the mean adjusts within the first five-minute bar while volatility stays elevated for about an hour; around FOMC announcements the excess variance of equity futures and exchange rates lasts ten hours or more ([Boehm and Kroner 2025](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/boehm-2025-fed-non-yield-shock.md)).
- US releases are the global risk impulse; they move stock indices of 27 countries within 30 minutes ([Boehm and Kroner 2023](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/boehm-2023-us-economic-news-global-financial-cycle.md)). When crypto responds, it responds as a high-beta risk asset: Bitcoin fell about 24 bp per standard deviation of inflation surprise in 2013 to 2021 ([Pinchuk 2023](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/pinchuk-2023-bitcoin-not-hedge-inflation.md)), and its 60-minute response to a one-standard-deviation CPI surprise went from about -4 bp to about -114 bp in 2021 to 2023, scaling with pre-release attention ([Kroner 2025](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/kroner-2025-macro-news-investor-attention.md)).
- The response is regime-specific. Bitcoin was statistically unresponsive to almost all US macro surprises in 2017 to 2022 in one study ([Benigno and Rosa 2023](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/benigno-2023-bitcoin-macro-disconnect.md)). A Federal Reserve staff study, known in this repository only through a news article, reports strong responses after 2021 with adjustment over 15 to 30 minutes ([Kroner et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/kroner-2026-cryptocurrencies-price-economic-news.md), secondary access, grade C).
- Volatility is the robust effect; direction is not. Five-minute volatility at least doubles for DeFi collateral tokens at the FOMC statement and again at the press conference ([Kyriazis et al. 2023](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/kyriazis-2023-monetary-policy-digital-assets-defi.md)). The sign of the response to growth news flips with the state of the cycle in equities ([Andersen et al. 2005](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/andersen-2005-real-time-price-discovery-stock-bond-fx.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: on event days the volatility and liquidity layer (1.7) is dominated by the calendar, and the equity link (1.3) carries the sign. With roughly 8 FOMC, 12 CPI and 12 payroll events a year there are about two hundred headline events in the post-2021 sample, too few for a model to learn responses end to end ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 1.3 The crypto market factor and cross-asset links (minutes to days)

- Crypto is close to a one-factor market. The first principal component of major-coin returns went from 36% of variance in 2015 to 2016 to above 80% in 2019 to 2020 ([Aslanidis et al. 2020](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/aslanidis-2020-cryptocurrencies-becoming-more-interconnected.md)).
- Liquidity decides who leads: the more actively traded instrument leads ([Huth and Abergel 2014](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/huth-2014-high-frequency-lead-lag-empirical-facts.md)). At one-minute resolution on Binance, Bitcoin leads only illiquid altcoins; ETH and LTC move inside the same minute ([Kurihara and Matsumoto 2026](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/kurihara-2026-price-transmission-bitcoin-altcoins.md)). SOL is not analysed in either study.
- No study in the repository measures a lead from equity index futures to BTC or SOL at seconds to minutes; the evidence is contemporaneous ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 21](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Volatility dynamics are largely common across coins: one model trained on a pooled panel of 213 coins beats per-coin models ([Tang et al. 2023](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/tang-2023-lstm-rough-volatility-crypto-winter.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: BTC and ETH on the large venues are the first-order input, and SOL-specific signals belong on the residual of SOL against the factor ([topic 02](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/SYNTHESIS.md)). Cross-asset information works as factor state (trend, flow and volatility of the market factor and of US equities, weighted by rolling betas), not as "trade SOL on BTC's last move" ([topic 21](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 1.4 Leverage, funding and positioning (hours to days)

- The perp is anchored to spot softly: it is pinned only inside a band set by arbitrage costs ([He et al. 2022](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/he-2022-fundamentals-perpetual-futures.md)). Positive funding is mostly leveraged long demand meeting limited arbitrage capital: carry fell when Binance cut maximum leverage, and over one 8-hour window funding is persistent and known in advance while the change in basis looks close to noise ([Christin et al. 2022](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/christin-2022-crypto-carry-trade.md)).
- Leverage accumulates in calm periods ([Thurner et al. 2010](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/thurner-2010-leverage-fat-tails-clustered-volatility.md)), and funding constraints and market liquidity reinforce each other through loss and margin spirals ([Brunnermeier and Pedersen 2009](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/brunnermeier-2009-market-liquidity-funding-liquidity.md)).
- Leverage build-up has not been shown to time a cascade. Across seven BTC cascades no single variable gives a consistent early warning ([Garcia Seuma 2026](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/garcia-seuma-2026-early-warning-liquidation-cascades.md), grade C, single author).
- What leverage does set is amplitude. Order flow moves the premium about three times more when the premium is already stretched ([He et al. 2022](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/he-2022-fundamentals-perpetual-futures.md)), and the size of a drop is set by flow relative to liquidity ([Donier and Bouchaud 2015](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/donier-2015-why-do-markets-crash-bitcoin.md)).
- Positioning in crypto is trend-confirming and lagging. Manual front-end traders on Hyperliquid open in the direction of recent returns while algorithmic participants open against them ([Jia et al. 2026](../topics/18-positioning-sentiment/notes/jia-2026-who-loses-why-perpetual-futures-hyperliquid.md)); retail brokerage clients who trade against moves in stocks and gold do not do so in crypto ([Kogan et al. 2023](../topics/18-positioning-sentiment/notes/kogan-2023-are-cryptos-different-retail.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: the stock of leverage and the stretch of the premium determine how far a given shock travels and whether the resulting flow is forced. They do not say when the shock comes.

### 1.5 SOL-specific flows and options (minutes to days)

- On-chain flow effects are short-lived and small. Bitcoin's response to a Tether mint peaks at 30 minutes and fades by 60 ([Saggu 2022](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/notes/saggu-2022-intraday-bitcoin-tether-minting-burning.md)); USDT exchange inflows predict returns at 1 and 2 hours but not at 3 to 6 ([Chi et al. 2024](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md), grade C). None of this was tested on SOL.
- Solana's on-chain activity is concentrated and regime-dependent ([Mancino 2025](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/notes/mancino-2025-memecoin-phenomenon-solana-pumpfun.md), [Blockworks 2026](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/notes/blockworks-2026-solana-tokenholder-report-q2.md)). The fast Solana-native series (priority fees, tips, failure rates) have never been tested against SOL returns in anything read ([topic 19](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- SOL options are too thin to carry signals of their own: about 76.5 million USD of Deribit open interest against 25.8 billion for BTC ([Deribit snapshot](../topics/20-options-derived-signals/notes/deribit-2026-option-book-summary-sol-liquidity.md)). Option-derived inputs must come from BTC and ETH, and option flow carries volatility information first ([Alexander et al. 2023](../topics/20-options-derived-signals/notes/alexander-2023-net-buying-pressure-bitcoin-option-trades.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: these are mainly risk flags (halts, stress in stablecoin premia) and volatility inputs.

### 1.6 The clock (deterministic)

- Second moments are strongly periodic. Volume, volatility and illiquidity trough at about 01:00 to 05:00 UTC and peak at about 14:00 to 17:00 UTC across 1,940 pairs on 38 exchanges ([Brauneis et al. 2025](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/brauneis-2025-crypto-world-trades-tea-time.md)). Weekends are quieter; CME's own figures put weekend volatility at about 75% of weekday volatility ([CME Group 2026](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/cmegroup-2026-aligning-crypto-derivatives-247-trading.md)). Volatility and volume jump in the first minute of each hour and after minutes 15, 30 and 45, most strongly at funding hours ([Hansen et al. 2021](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/hansen-2021-periodicity-crypto-volatility-liquidity.md)).
- Around each 8-hour funding settlement, volume rises by about 50%, spreads widen by about 3% and order-flow toxicity rises by about 17% ([funding-cycle paper](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/anonymous-2025-perpetual-futures-funding-cycle-market-quality.md); see also [Ruan and Streltsov 2024](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/ruan-2024-perpetual-futures-market-quality.md)).
- Mean-return seasonality is weak and unstable in crypto ([Baur et al. 2019](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/baur-2019-bitcoin-time-of-day-day-of-week.md), [Kinateder and Papavassiliou 2021](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/kinateder-2021-calendar-effects-bitcoin-returns-volatility.md)) and in traditional markets, where a five-fold range in intraday volatility coexists with no systematic pattern in mean returns ([Andersen and Bollerslev 1997](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/notes/andersen-1997-intraday-periodicity-volatility-persistence.md)).
- Where directional clock patterns exist in traditional markets, they come from predictable, price-insensitive flow meeting limited intermediary capacity: a drift into the liquidity event and a reversal after it. The conditioning variables carry the information, not the clock ([topic 11](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/SYNTHESIS.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: the clock scales volatility, volume, spread and toxicity, so every fast input should be deseasonalised. It also marks when scheduled flow arrives, which decides when a flow measurement is informative (see the quarter-hour result in 1.8).

### 1.7 Volatility and liquidity state (minutes to hours)

- Volatility is measurable and forecastable, and a three-scale autoregression on realised variance (HAR) is the benchmark that is hard to beat by much ([Corsi 2009](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/corsi-2009-har-long-memory-realized-volatility.md), [topic 14](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/SYNTHESIS.md)). In Bitcoin, jumps cluster and are followed by about an hour of elevated volatility and volume ([Scaillet et al. 2020](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/scaillet-2020-high-frequency-jump-analysis-bitcoin.md)).
- Impact is concave in size and scales with volatility and volume, close to `Y * sigma * sqrt(Q / V)`, and it is transient ([Toth et al. 2011](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/notes/toth-2011-anomalous-impact-critical-liquidity.md), [Bouchaud et al. 2008](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/notes/bouchaud-2008-markets-slowly-digest-supply-demand.md)). The practical unit of impact is `sigma / sqrt(V)`.
- Liquidity conditions predict volatility better than direction: microstructure liquidity measures predict the sign of the change in realised volatility for SOL and four other coins with accuracy of 0.56 to 0.58 ([Easley et al. 2024](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/notes/easley-2024-microstructure-dynamics-crypto-markets.md)).
- Inside a cascade the signature is in liquidity. On Hyperliquid on 10 October 2025 the median inside spread across five majors rose more than 25-fold in the peak hour and was back near 1 bp two hours later, while depth stayed impaired for weeks ([Lim 2026](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/lim-2026-liquidity-recovery-hyperliquid-liquidation-cascade.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: this state converts a given amount of flow into a size of move, sets the cost of trading, and sets the reward for fading. In equities, short-term reversal returns are strongly predicted by the VIX ([Nagel 2012](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/nagel-2012-evaporating-liquidity.md)).

### 1.8 Trade flow and shocks (minutes to hours): where direction is decided

- A price move has a permanent part and a transitory part, and the transitory part is payment for absorbing flow ([Hendershott and Menkveld 2014](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/hendershott-2014-price-pressures.md)). Volume does not sign the trade: high-volume moves reverse in liquid large caps and continue where information asymmetry is high ([Llorente et al. 2002](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/llorente-2002-dynamic-volume-return-relation.md)). Hedging or forced trades reverse; informed trades continue.
- Flow pressure is absorbed in minutes in a liquid market, and the window shrinks as markets get faster ([Chordia et al. 2005](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/chordia-2005-speed-convergence-market-efficiency.md)). Flow is not always transitory: a common order-flow factor explains most of the common price move across Bitcoin venues ([Makarov and Schoar 2020](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/makarov-2020-trading-arbitrage-cryptocurrency-markets.md)).
- Signed flow is highly persistent and that persistence is already priced ([Lillo and Farmer 2004](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/lillo-2004-long-memory-efficient-market.md), [Bouchaud et al. 2004](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/bouchaud-2004-fluctuations-response-financial-markets.md)). A high taker-buy ratio mostly forecasts more taker buying.
- In crypto the unconditional intraday tendency is reversal, growing with the size of the preceding move: first-order autocorrelation of -0.056 at one hour, rising in magnitude to -0.19 after five-sigma one-hour bars ([De Nicola 2021](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/denicola-2021-intraday-behavior-bitcoin.md), Bitstamp 2015 to 2018, in-sample, no costs).
- Conditioning creates what directional content there is. At 15 minutes, reversal appears in 90% of 183 Binance pairs and concentrates after moves that taker flow pushed; the gross edge peaks near 1.3 bp against a 5 bp round trip ([Kitron and Wengrowicz 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kitron-2026-short-horizon-mean-reversion-crypto.md)). Taker imbalance in the first 10 seconds of a quarter-hour predicts returns 4 to 12 hours ahead, with a negative slope in the first half hour; SOL is the weakest of six contracts ([Kim and Hansen 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kim-2026-quarter-hour-effect-crypto-futures.md)).
- Forced flow is fast and overshoots. On 10 October 2025, 88% of post-onset forced selling on Hyperliquid landed within 30 minutes ([Garcia Seuma 2026](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/garcia-seuma-2026-branching-ratio-liquidation-cascade.md)). Perpetuals overshoot spot by about 3% of a large move and the gap closes within hours ([Gornall et al. 2025](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/gornall-2025-perpetual-futures-basis-risk.md)).

How it conditions the next layer: this is where the slow state becomes a signed, short-lived forecast. The forecast is small relative to the cost of acting on it, which hands the problem to execution.

### 1.9 Order book and venue transmission (milliseconds to seconds)

- Order flow imbalance explains contemporaneous price change (about 65% of 10-second mid-price variance in US equities, [Cont et al. 2014](../topics/07-order-book-imbalance/notes/cont-2014-price-impact-order-book-events.md)), and its predictive power decays within seconds to a few minutes. On the Binance BTC perpetual a book model's F1 falls from about 75 at roughly one second to about 41 at roughly ten seconds ([Berti and Kasneci 2025](../topics/22-deep-learning-lob/notes/berti-2025-tlob-dual-attention-transformer.md)).
- The edge is smaller than the cost of crossing: about +1 bp per round trip before fees on the Binance BTC perp, negative after a 1.5 bp taker fee per leg, measured with live orders ([Albers et al. 2025](../topics/07-order-book-imbalance/notes/albers-2025-market-makers-dilemma-fill-probability.md)).
- Binance leads Hyperliquid in 29 of 29 assets including SOL ([Nayyar 2026](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/nayyar-2026-hyperliquid-lags-binance-lead-lag.md), grade C). Lags between liquid venues are tens of milliseconds to about a second. A visible lag is not an edge for an ordinary participant: laggards are predictable with high R-squared yet taker PnL is negative at default fees ([Albers et al. 2021](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/albers-2021-fragmentation-price-formation-cross-impact.md)), and a walk-forward model could not beat a small follower venue's own mid ([Young 2026](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/young-2026-openmarket-polymarket-binance-dataset.md)).
- The "perp leads" result is a calm-market result. In a liquidation cascade the Binance perp first responded perversely and then overshot, and an accuracy-based metric credited spot with leadership ([Shen et al. 2026](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/shen-2026-misguided-price-discovery-overshooting.md)).
- Kalshi's anchor is a US spot index, so offshore lead-lag reaches Kalshi through US-accessible spot books ([SOLPERP filing](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-solperp-contract-terms-cftc-filing.md)). In a one-day snapshot the Kalshi book's one-minute returns had a 0.976 correlation with Coinbase spot and about -0.01 at a one-minute lead or lag ([snapshot](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-solperp-public-market-data-snapshot.md)).

### How the layers combine (reading)

The notes do not test the layers jointly. Put side by side, they imply that the same observable event means different things in different states. Take a fast 1% fall in SOL on the large venues.

- If open interest falls with one-sided taker selling in a thin overnight hour, the flow is forced, most of it lands within half an hour, and the literature points to partial reversal once it stops (1.4, 1.7, 1.8).
- If it follows a hawkish surprise at 8:30 ET in a high-funding, high-equity-beta regime, it is the repricing of a common factor, volatility stays high for an hour or more, and nothing in the notes supports fading it (1.1, 1.2, 1.3).
- If it is SOL-only with BTC and ETH flat, it is a residual move, and the relevant evidence is on venue-specific and uncorrelated flow, which tends to revert ([Makarov and Schoar 2020](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/makarov-2020-trading-arbitrage-cryptocurrency-markets.md), [Donier and Bonart 2015](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/notes/donier-2015-million-metaorder-bitcoin-impact.md)).

The forced-versus-informed classifier that this implies is graded D in [topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md): it is a hypothesis built from theory and has not been tested on crypto.

## 3. Contradictions between topics and how to read them

**1. High volume: follow or fade?** [Topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md) reads four settings as "high relative volume means follow, low relative volume means fade" ([Bianchi et al. 2022](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/notes/bianchi-2022-trading-volume-liquidity-provision-crypto.md), [Shen et al. 2022](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/notes/shen-2022-bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum.md)). [Topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md) starts from the opposite classic result, that moves on high volume reverse more ([Campbell et al. 1993](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/campbell-1993-trading-volume-serial-correlation-returns.md)), and [topic 08](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/SYNTHESIS.md) finds 15-minute reversal concentrated after moves pushed by aggressive taker flow ([Kitron and Wengrowicz 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kitron-2026-short-horizon-mean-reversion-crypto.md)). Reading: [Llorente et al. 2002](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/llorente-2002-dynamic-volume-return-relation.md) is the hinge. Volume from informed trading continues and volume from hedging or forced trading reverses, so volume does not sign the trade until the flow is classified. The topic 13 evidence is mostly daily and cross-sectional across coins of different liquidity; the topic 08 evidence is 15-minute and within one liquid venue. Do not carry one rule across horizons.

**2. Follow flow or fade it, by horizon.** Reversal after flow-driven 15-minute bars, reversal after large one- and two-hour bars ([De Nicola 2021](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/denicola-2021-intraday-behavior-bitcoin.md)), continuation over 4 to 12 hours after quarter-hour boundary flow ([Kim and Hansen 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kim-2026-quarter-hour-effect-crypto-futures.md)), continuation for days to about two to four weeks in trend studies ([topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md)), and a contrarian sign for oscillators on 5-minute bars against a losing fade on daily bars ([topic 16](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/SYNTHESIS.md)). DeMark evidence points the same way: follow the completed setup, do not fade the countdown ([topic 15](../topics/15-demark-indicators/SYNTHESIS.md)). Reading: these are consistent with reversal at the shortest lags and continuation from several hours outward, but nobody has located the crossover, and for SOL the horizon map in [topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md) (open question 1) is the first experiment to run.

**3. Funding: crowding gauge, macro-sensitivity regime, or nothing?** [Topic 03](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/SYNTHESIS.md) lists funding in excess of the default as a grade B crowding measure and [topic 02](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/SYNTHESIS.md) uses a funding regime to scale the response to Fed news ([Copestake et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/copestake-2026-crypto-cycle-institutional-investors.md)). [Topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md) concludes funding "mostly echoes the recent price path" with no demonstrated short-horizon predictive content. Reading: the claims differ in what is predicted. Funding describes the state of leverage (B) and may scale responses; it has not been shown to predict the sign of the next move. Copestake's own caveat is that high funding partly means "after a rally".

**4. The lag is real, the lag is not an edge, and the lag may not be there.** [Topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md) and [topic 21](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/SYNTHESIS.md) agree that small venues lag and that a taker cannot harvest the lag. Topic 21's "liquidity law" predicts a Kalshi lag behind offshore venues that the major venues no longer show. The only Kalshi measurement, in [topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md), finds a one-minute lead or lag correlation with Coinbase of about zero. Reading: no conflict. Kalshi's lag, if any, is inside a minute, which makes it an execution matter (do not rest stale quotes, do not chase a move the makers have priced) and not a source of minutes-to-hours alpha.

**5. Which index anchors Kalshi?** [Topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md) inferred Kalshi's terms from the NEAR and BNB filings and lists six constituents for the Solana index, including Crypto.com, from the CF Benchmarks constituent document ([CF Benchmarks note](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/cfbenchmarks-2026-cme-cf-real-time-indices-methodology.md)). [Topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md) read the SOL filing itself, which names five ([SOLPERP filing](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-solperp-contract-terms-cftc-filing.md)). Reading: topic 06 supersedes topic 05 on contract terms; the constituent list should be checked against the current CF Benchmarks document before building an index proxy.

**6. Trade passively, but passive fills lose.** [Topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md)'s illustrative break-even table says a minutes-horizon strategy on Kalshi's entry tier is viable only if most volume executes passively. The same topic and [topic 07](../topics/07-order-book-imbalance/SYNTHESIS.md) show that live passive orders on a crypto perp fill mostly when the next seconds go against them ([Albers et al. 2025](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/notes/albers-2025-market-makers-dilemma-fill-probability.md)), while imbalance-conditioned quoting worked on 2014 Nasdaq data ([Cartea et al. 2018](../topics/07-order-book-imbalance/notes/cartea-2018-enhancing-trading-order-book-signals.md)). Reading: both are true. The decision is an expected-value comparison of fee saved, adverse markout if filled and the value of the trade missed if not filled, and its inputs are unknown for Kalshi. On the Binance BTC perp the experiment's orders had an unconditional expected five-second return of about -0.8 bp; on Kalshi the gap between taker and maker fees is 10 bp. That arithmetic favours resting orders only if Kalshi's markout is of the same order, which has not been measured.

**7. Regime: gate, feature or selector?** [Topic 01](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/SYNTHESIS.md) supports cutting exposure in turbulent states and warns that regime labels say little about direction. [Topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md) lists regime-specialised sub-policies as a positive RL result ([Zong et al. 2024](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/zong-2024-macrohft-memory-context-rl-crypto.md)); [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md) reports that the same agent lost 75% on ETH when re-run in a different environment ([FineFT](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/qin-2025-fineft-ensemble-rl-crypto-futures.md)); [topic 29](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/SYNTHESIS.md) finds that selecting among experts is beaten by blending. Reading: use regime as a risk scaler and as an input feature. Do not let it hard-switch between learned strategies.

**8. The clock does not predict returns, except when it does.** Topics [10](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/SYNTHESIS.md), [11](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/SYNTHESIS.md) and [12](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/SYNTHESIS.md) agree that clock features support volatility, volume and cost models and not direction. Yet the quarter-hour boundary result ([Kim and Hansen 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kim-2026-quarter-hour-effect-crypto-futures.md)) and session intraday momentum ([Shen et al. 2022](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/shen-2022-bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum.md), grade A, break-even cost 3 to 10 bp) are clock-anchored return effects. Reading: in both cases the clock marks when a particular kind of flow arrives, and the flow or the session return is the predictor. That matches topic 11's finding that the conditioning variable carries the information.

**9. Macro matters a great deal and macro features do not help.** Topics [02](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/SYNTHESIS.md) and [30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md) document large post-2021 responses to US news and a state-dependent equity link, and both report that macro inputs add little to learned models ([Dudek et al. 2025](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/dudek-2025-bitcoin-volatility-gradient-boosting.md), [Sanhueza 2026](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/sanhueza-2026-bitcoin-return-predictability-comprehensive.md)). Reading: macro acts contemporaneously and on known dates. It belongs in the bot as an event schedule, a volatility multiplier and a regime descriptor, not as a lagged predictor.

**10. Refit often, and refitting hurts.** Short training windows and incremental updates win in [topic 23](../topics/23-ml-feature-prediction/SYNTHESIS.md); crypto order-book models decay to chance without frequent retraining in one study while equity models stay stable for 18 months ([topic 22](../topics/22-deep-learning-lob/SYNTHESIS.md)). [Topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md) shows that refitting every period and trading the newest rule gave the best gross and the worst net return ([Martin et al. 2019](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/martin-2019-grammatical-evolution-ensembles-algorithmic-trading.md)), and [topic 26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md) counts every refit configuration as a trial. Reading: refit on a schedule fixed in advance, change position only when several recent fits agree, and log the refits.

**11. Overfitting gates.** Topics [16](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/SYNTHESIS.md) and [26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md) recommend the probability of backtest overfitting; [topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md) notes that the gate accepted the agent that then performed worst ([Gort et al. 2022](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/gort-2022-drl-crypto-backtest-overfitting.md)). Reading: the measure checks rank stability among trials, not profitability. It is necessary, not sufficient.

**12. Does an economic story protect a signal?** [Topic 11](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/SYNTHESIS.md) separates calendar effects with a mechanism (turn of month) from those without (day of week). [Topic 26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md) reports that theory-backed predictors survive no better than mined ones ([Chen et al. 2022](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/chen-2022-peer-reviewed-research-predict-returns.md)). Reading: a mechanism tells you which conditioning variable to test and when the effect should switch off; it is not a substitute for out-of-sample evidence.

**13. Grades differ for the same paper.** The same work is noted under several topics with different grades (for example [Albers et al. 2021](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/albers-2021-fragmentation-price-formation-cross-impact.md) is B in topic 05 and C in [topic 08](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/albers-2021-fragmentation-cross-impact-bitcoin.md); FineFT is B in topics 03, 04 and 31 and C in topic 14). Reading: a grade refers to the use made of the source in that topic. Check the note in the topic you are working in.

**14. Volume is an input everywhere and volume data are suspect.** Volume conditions signals in topics 13, 14 and 28. [Topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md) reports estimated wash trading above 70% of reported volume on unregulated exchanges in 2019 ([Cong et al. 2023](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/cong-2023-crypto-wash-trading.md)), [topic 04](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/SYNTHESIS.md) shows exchange open interest failing a consistency test against volume ([Giagkiozis and Said 2024](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/giagkiozis-2024-reconciling-open-interest-traded-volume.md)), and on Kalshi daily volume is about 12 times open interest during rebate programmes ([snapshot](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-solperp-public-market-data-snapshot.md)). Reading: audit volume by venue before using it, and do not read Kalshi volume as depth.

## 4. What the Kalshi venue changes

Facts in this section are from [topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md) unless linked otherwise. The venue has almost no independent research; most of what is known is documentation plus one day of data pulled on 2026-11-22.

### Leverage cap

- Leverage near 3x means local liquidation clusters sit further from the mark than on offshore venues, so local cascades should be rarer and smaller and will mostly arrive through the reference price ([Kalshi margin note](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-margin-liquidation-docs.md), an inference stated there).
- The true liquidation distance for a SOL position is unknown without authenticated endpoints: whether the published leverage estimate uses initial or maintenance margin is not confirmed. The documentation does not say whether auto-deleveraging exists or how losses are allocated if liquidations fail ([Kalshi margin note](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-margin-liquidation-docs.md)).
- Longs and shorts get different leverage, so sizing is side-asymmetric.
- Volatility targeting asks for more notional when volatility is low; how often the cap binds is an open question in [topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md). For scale, extreme-value estimates on Bitcoin perpetuals put survivable leverage near 3x to 5x for a 1% daily margin-call probability ([Cheng et al. 2021](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/cheng-2021-liquidation-leverage-optimal-margin-bitcoin.md)); SOL was not tested.
- If funding is the price of leverage ([Christin et al. 2022](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/christin-2022-crypto-carry-trade.md)), a low cap predicts small funding; thin arbitrage capital on a new venue predicts wider, slower-closing gaps to the index. Which dominates is open ([topic 03](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/SYNTHESIS.md)). The observed funding history is consistent with the first.
- Exit triggers fire as reduce-only IOC limit orders that can fill partially or not at all, and a stop placed beyond the estimated liquidation price is rejected ([API note](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-rest-websocket-fix.md), [Kalshi margin note](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-margin-liquidation-docs.md)). A stop is not a guaranteed exit.

### Funding schedule

- Funding was exactly zero in 401 of 517 intervals since launch, with a mean of about 0.15 bp per 8 hours and a largest magnitude of 8.5 bp ([snapshot](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-solperp-public-market-data-snapshot.md)). The premium registers only when the index falls outside the $1,000 impact bid and ask. As a holding cost, funding on Kalshi is a step function that is usually zero, not a linear carry term.
- The binding formula weights each second's premium in proportion to its position in the interval, so the last two hours carry about 44% of the rate ([SOLPERP filing](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-solperp-contract-terms-cftc-filing.md)). The help center and the API reference describe the average differently; the filing is the legal text and the rule can be settled empirically ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md), open question 1).
- Kalshi's funding clock is in Eastern Time: 04:00, 12:00 and 20:00 UTC in summer and 05:00, 13:00 and 21:00 UTC after the clock change. The offshore 8-hour clock is 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC ([topic 12](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/SYNTHESIS.md)). So the documented activity bursts at offshore funding times (1.6) fall four or five hours away from Kalshi's own settlements (this document's arithmetic). Hyperliquid funds hourly ([Hyperliquid specification](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/hyperliquid-2026-funding-rate-specification.md)). A bot needs a funding-phase feature per venue, and should read `next_funding_time` from the API instead of hard-coding it.
- Funding obligations are computed at the interval end and paid at the next settlement time (12:00 PM and 4:00 PM ET).
- Because Kalshi funding is mostly zero, it carries little information about leverage. The leverage state has to be read from offshore funding and premium ([He et al. 2022](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/he-2022-fundamentals-perpetual-futures.md), [Binance specification](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/binance-2026-funding-rate-specification.md)).
- Competing US SOL perps (Coinbase Derivatives, Bitnomial, OG.com) have funding clocks in other time zones or hourly funding; whether staggered boundaries produce flow on Kalshi is a grade D hypothesis ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- If the funding rate cannot be determined, including for insufficient book liquidity, it is set to zero, and in an emergency the exchange may change the maximum funding magnitude and margin ([SOLPERP filing](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-solperp-contract-terms-cftc-filing.md)).

### Costs and liquidity

- A tier-0 taker round trip is about 24 bp in fees plus a 4.5 bp spread, against one-minute volatility near 8 bp. A maker round trip is 4 bp. The taker fee falls to 6 bp at $1M of 30-day volume and to 2 bp at $10M ([fee schedule](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshiex-2026-perpetuals-fee-schedule-amendment.md)).
- Self-clearing members pay 4 bp and 2 bp and receive a 98% taker-fee rebate through 31 December 2026. A retail bot's cost is several times that of the firms it trades against, and spreads may change when the rebate ends.
- [Topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md) works an illustrative break-even from these inputs (its author's arithmetic, not a sourced result): with a forecast correlation of 0.05, trading every signal, the break-even holding horizon is about five days with taker execution both ways and about two and a half to five and a half hours with maker execution both ways, depending on the adverse selection assumed. The qualitative message is robust: most volume must execute passively, or the bot must trade rarely and only on its strongest forecasts.
- Liquidity on the snapshot day: median spread 4.5 bp (maximum 8.1 bp); about $20k per side within 5 bp of mid and $144k to $487k within 10 bp; open interest $1.43M; 24-hour volume $17.2M; 214 of 1,440 minutes with no trade ([snapshot](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-solperp-public-market-data-snapshot.md)). Clip sizes of $1,000 to $20,000 fit the book; above roughly $100,000 the impact price moves. This is one day and one book.
- Fee schedules, rebate programmes, leverage calibrations and the number of competing venues all changed within five months of launch. Backtests must use dated fee regimes.

### Data access

- Public market data needs no authentication: order book, trades with taker side, one-minute candles with bid and ask, funding history, a per-second premium index, risk parameters. REST, WebSocket and FIX are available, and a demo exchange mirrors the API ([API note](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-rest-websocket-fix.md)).
- History is short: one-minute bars and trades go back to June 2026, about 248,000 minutes. That is small for deep models and smaller still for walk-forward validation, so the realistic route is to pretrain or fit on offshore data and calibrate on Kalshi ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- The per-second premium index is not retained; record it from now on.
- The public trade feed has no liquidation flag and the margin API has no queue positions ([Kalshi margin note](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-margin-liquidation-docs.md), [API note](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/notes/kalshi-2026-perps-api-rest-websocket-fix.md)). Local forced flow must be inferred, and passive fill models cannot be checked against queue data.
- The Basic tier allows about 20 order writes per second, ample for this horizon.

## 5. A suggested architecture

This section is a synthesis, not a tested design. Every component links to the notes that motivate it, and the notes test none of the components jointly. The governing idea from sections 1 to 3: slow inputs and the clock produce a conditioning state; flow and technical inputs produce small signed forecasts; a cost-aware rule turns the blend into a position; execution and risk control sit outside whatever is learned.

```
SLOW STATE (hours to days)        CLOCK (deterministic)          FAST STATE (seconds to minutes)
regime and risk appetite          time of day and week           volatility forecast, jump flag
equity beta, market-mode share    funding phase per venue        spread, depth, impact unit
crypto factor trend               minute of hour                 forced-flow and stress gauges
offshore funding and premium      event calendar
          \                              |                              /
           +-------------------> conditioning state <------------------+
                                         |
DIRECTIONAL COMPONENTS: each a standardised forecast with an estimated decay rate
trend and factor state | flow and shock response | venue basis | technical features | event response
                                         |
COMBINATION: trim, equal-risk blend within and across families, slow shrunk tilt
                                         |
POSITION RULE: no-trade band, volatility target, leverage and liquidation-distance cap
                                         |
EXECUTION: rest or cross, cancel on lead-venue move, small state-conditioned policy
                                         |
RISK OVERLAY, outside every learned component: liquidity circuit breaker, novelty gate,
drawdown and time-under-water limits, event and maintenance windows
```

### 5.1 Data hygiene

- Align venues on event time; feed latency alone can manufacture a lead ([topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)). Use the Hayashi-Yoshida estimator on raw timestamps for lag measurement ([Hoffmann et al. 2013](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/hoffmann-2013-lead-lag-non-synchronous-data.md)).
- Sign trades with the exchange's aggressor flag before aggregating ([Andersen and Bondarenko 2013](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/andersen-2013-order-flow-toxicity-perfect-classification.md)).
- Filter open interest for consistency with volume, and treat liquidation totals as lower bounds ([Giagkiozis and Said 2024](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/giagkiozis-2024-reconciling-open-interest-traded-volume.md), [Binance stream docs](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/binance-2026-liquidation-order-stream-docs.md)).
- Compute every filtered or decomposed feature on trailing data only; decomposition before splitting produced error reductions above 90% that vanished under causal processing ([Jiang et al. 2024](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/notes/jiang-2024-signal-decomposition-leakage-time-series-prediction.md)).
- Use point-in-time contract specifications, fee schedules and funding; the perp evaluation checklist is in [Zhu and Cai 2026](../topics/23-ml-feature-prediction/notes/zhu-2026-ai-equity-crypto-markets-profitability-limits.md) and the strict backtest semantics in [Deng 2025](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/deng-2025-autoquant-execution-constrained-crypto-perps.md).

### 5.2 Regime inputs

- Purpose: scale risk and condition other components. A regime label is a reliable guide to risk and a poor guide to direction ([topic 01](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Start with a plain realised-volatility threshold. It is the baseline that none of the regime sources tests against ([topic 01](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/SYNTHESIS.md), open question 2). Then compare a filtered HMM and a statistical jump model with a persistence device ([Shu et al. 2024](../topics/01-macro-regime-detection/notes/shu-2024-downside-risk-statistical-jump-model.md)). Only filtered, never-revised labels count.
- Add daily state variables as features: rolling equity beta and R-squared in US hours ([Drexler et al. 2026](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/drexler-2026-bitcoin-rising-beta-equities.md)), the first-eigenvalue share of major-coin correlations ([Aslanidis et al. 2020](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/aslanidis-2020-cryptocurrencies-becoming-more-interconnected.md)), a risk-on/risk-off index ([Chari et al. 2023](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/notes/chari-2023-risk-on-risk-off-multifaceted.md)), and a funding regime built from offshore funding ([Copestake et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/copestake-2026-crypto-cycle-institutional-investors.md)).
- Scheduled events enter as hand-specified features: time to and since release, standardised surprise once known, release category, and a post-release volatility multiplier, with coefficients estimated by pooled regression across BTC, ETH and SOL ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 5.3 Time-of-day and time-of-week inputs

- Purpose: deseasonalise, and scale volatility, size and cost. Clock features have strong support as inputs to volatility, volume and cost models and weak support as return predictors ([topic 10](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Encoding by model class ([topic 12](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/SYNTHESIS.md), which gives the formulas): integer minute-of-day and day-of-week for tree models; about four daily harmonics, three weekly harmonics and a funding-phase pair for linear and neural models; a New York local-time pair for daylight-saving shifts.
- Fit the volatility seasonal with a Fourier flexible form on trailing data and divide returns by it ([Andersen and Bollerslev 1997](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/notes/andersen-1997-intraday-periodicity-volatility-persistence.md)); the amplitude drifts, so re-estimate on a rolling window ([Vatter et al. 2013](../topics/12-time-encodings-cycles/notes/vatter-2013-intraday-spot-volatility-trend-seasonality.md)).
- Carry separate funding phases for Kalshi (Eastern Time), offshore 8-hour venues (UTC) and Hyperliquid (hourly), plus minute-of-hour and quarter-hour flags ([Hansen et al. 2021](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/notes/hansen-2021-periodicity-crypto-volatility-liquidity.md)).
- Every clock-based return feature must pass an out-of-sample ablation and a search-adjusted test. A search over 24 hours times 7 days times several regimes will always find a profitable cell in-sample ([topic 10](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/SYNTHESIS.md), [Sullivan et al. 2001](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/notes/sullivan-2001-dangers-data-mining-calendar-effects.md)).

### 5.4 Volatility and liquidity state

- A HAR forecast on deseasonalised log realised variance, with a jump flag and an hour of post-jump elevation, estimated on the lead venue ([Corsi 2009](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/corsi-2009-har-long-memory-realized-volatility.md), [Scaillet et al. 2020](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/scaillet-2020-high-frequency-jump-analysis-bitcoin.md)). Intraday HAR on crypto perps is essentially untested ([topic 14](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/SYNTHESIS.md)), so this is a first experiment, not a given.
- Spread and depth ratios to their same-hour baselines, carried separately because depth recovers more slowly ([Lim 2026](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/lim-2026-liquidity-recovery-hyperliquid-liquidation-cascade.md)); the impact unit `sigma / sqrt(V)` ([topic 09](../topics/09-liquidity-price-impact/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Implied volatility from BTC and ETH options as a prior ([topic 20](../topics/20-options-derived-signals/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 5.5 Flow inputs

- Cross-venue signed taker flow, summed or as a first principal component, on the large venues ([Makarov and Schoar 2020](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/makarov-2020-trading-arbitrage-cryptocurrency-markets.md)).
- Flow-conditioned reversal at 15 minutes ([Kitron and Wengrowicz 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kitron-2026-short-horizon-mean-reversion-crypto.md)) and quarter-hour boundary imbalance for the 4 to 12 hour horizon ([Kim and Hansen 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/kim-2026-quarter-hour-effect-crypto-futures.md)), each re-tested on SOL, where the published result is weakest.
- Shock response: size of the last bar in volatility units ([De Nicola 2021](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/denicola-2021-intraday-behavior-bitcoin.md)), a forced-versus-informed split from open interest change and one-sided taker volume (grade D hypothesis, [topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md)), and a speed-of-move split.
- Venue basis: Kalshi mid against a cross-venue reference, and Kalshi-specific signed flow ([topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md), open question 3). Venue-specific deviations reverted on spot venues in 2017 to 2018.
- Order-book imbalance and micro-price from the lead venues, used for entry timing and quote protection, not for direction at this horizon ([topic 07](../topics/07-order-book-imbalance/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 22](../topics/22-deep-learning-lob/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Placebo and benchmark tests for every flow feature: lagged volatility and volume controls and a flow-shuffle null ([Jeon 2026](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/jeon-2026-order-flow-liquidity-state-crypto-futures.md), [Andersen and Bondarenko 2014](../topics/08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/andersen-2014-vpin-flash-crash.md)).

### 5.6 Technical inputs

- The slow items have the better evidence and tolerate fees: volatility-scaled trend over several look-backs, channel breakout context, hourly moving-average state, price-to-moving-average ratios ([topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 16](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/SYNTHESIS.md), [Mroziewicz and Slepaczuk 2026](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/notes/mroziewicz-2026-walk-forward-ema-intraday-bitcoin.md)).
- Fast oscillators (band z-scores, RSI) have a measurable gross effect that a taker cannot keep: the best 5-minute rule broke even at 6.32 bp one way ([Bakker 2017](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/notes/bakker-2017-intraday-technical-bitcoin.md)). Use them as continuous features or timing filters inside a slower view.
- DeMark counts, round-number proximity and rolling-extreme levels can be encoded as bounded features ([topic 15](../topics/15-demark-indicators/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 17](../topics/17-auction-orderflow-practitioner/SYNTHESIS.md)). Test them against matched placebo levels, since random levels already "bounce" 56% of the time ([Osler 2000](../topics/17-auction-orderflow-practitioner/notes/osler-2000-support-resistance-intraday-exchange-rates.md)).
- SOL's residual against BTC and ETH is the natural object for mean-reversion features ([Avellaneda and Lee 2010](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/notes/avellaneda-2010-statistical-arbitrage-us-equities.md)); crypto pairs backtests as published are not credible ([topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 5.7 One decision process

1. **Standardise and combine.** Scale each component forecast to unit volatility, trim, and take equal-risk weights within signal families and across families. Add only a slow, shrunk tilt from discounted performance, and require each added layer to beat the previous one net of costs ([topic 29](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/SYNTHESIS.md), [Timmermann 2006](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/notes/timmermann-2006-forecast-combinations-handbook.md)). Conditioning state enters as interactions with components, or as inputs to a shallow model, not as a switch.
2. **Weight by decay.** Fast-decaying predictors deserve less weight once costs are counted ([Garleanu and Pedersen 2009](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/notes/garleanu-2009-dynamic-trading-predictable-returns-costs.md)).
3. **Trade through a band.** With linear costs and a position cap, the optimal policy changes position only when the forecast crosses a threshold ([de Lataillade et al. 2012](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/notes/delataillade-2012-optimal-trading-linear-costs.md)). Turnover is a decision variable.
4. **Size by volatility, inside hard limits.** Volatility-target sizing for risk control ([Harvey et al. 2018](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/harvey-2018-impact-volatility-targeting.md)), a fractional Kelly ceiling because over-betting is worse than under-betting ([MacLean et al. 2010](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/maclean-2010-good-bad-properties-kelly-criterion.md)), and a liquidation-distance constraint from return extremes ([Cheng et al. 2021](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/notes/cheng-2021-liquidation-leverage-optimal-margin-bitcoin.md)), all capped by Kalshi's side-specific leverage.
5. **Execute by expected value.** Rest or cross according to fee saved, expected markout given a fill and the value of a missed trade; cancel resting orders when the lead venue moves ([Albers et al. 2025](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/notes/albers-2025-market-makers-dilemma-fill-probability.md), [topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md)).
6. **Keep risk control outside the learned parts.** A liquidity circuit breaker on spread and depth, a novelty gate that goes flat when the state is unfamiliar ([FineFT](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/qin-2025-fineft-ensemble-rl-crypto-futures.md)), drawdown and time-under-water limits ([Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/bailey-2014-stop-outs-serial-correlation-triple-penance.md)), and per-signal drift detectors that can halve or zero a weight ([Gama et al. 2014](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/notes/gama-2014-survey-concept-drift-adaptation.md)).

### 5.9 Where reinforcement learning fits, and where the notes warn against it

Fits:

- **As a formulation, even when no RL algorithm is used.** Previous position in the state, cost of changing position in the reward, and a risk-adjusted objective. For a perpetual the per-step reward is `price change x previous position - (half-spread + fee) x |position change| - funding x position` ([topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md), [Moody and Saffell 1998](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/moody-1998-reinforcement-learning-for-trading.md), [Borrageiro et al. 2022](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/borrageiro-2022-recurrent-rl-crypto-agent-perpetual.md)). On Kalshi, funding in that reward is a deadband step function evaluated three times a day ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- **Execution, where the bot's orders change what happens next.** A tabular policy on time left, inventory left, spread and cost to cross cut cost by a quarter to a half against a fixed limit price ([Nevmyvaka et al. 2006](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/nevmyvaka-2006-reinforcement-learning-optimized-trade-execution.md), grade A, equities, simulated impact).
- **Confined roles.** The credible positive results restrict the policy: timing and early exit inside fixed stops and limits ([Lebiedz and Slepaczuk 2026](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/lebiedz-2026-drl-execution-overlay-crypto-pair-trading.md)), or a fallback to a conservative policy in unfamiliar states ([FineFT](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/qin-2025-fineft-ensemble-rl-crypto-futures.md)).
- **A direct-reinforcement policy with online updates and a net-reward gate as a challenger** to the forecast-plus-band rule ([Borrageiro et al. 2022](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/borrageiro-2022-recurrent-rl-crypto-agent-perpetual.md), a single-path result).
- **Evaluation discipline.** Many seeds, bootstrap intervals, collapse checks, a blind holdout scored once ([Henderson et al. 2017](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/henderson-2017-deep-reinforcement-learning-that-matters.md), [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)).

Warnings:

- A small directional trader is a price taker. When actions do not move prices, multi-period credit assignment adds little beyond what the position-change cost already requires ([Ma 2025](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/ma-2025-myopic-optimality-rl-portfolio-lose-money.md), theory only). RL's machinery earns its keep where actions change future state: inventory, fills, queue position, impact, liquidation risk.
- Off-the-shelf deep RL loses on minute-level crypto, and published agents do not survive a change of environment: DQN, DDQN and PPO lost money at a 0.02% fee and a simple order-imbalance rule beat all three on three of four coins ([Zong et al. 2024](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/notes/zong-2024-macrohft-memory-context-rl-crypto.md)); the same paper's own method lost 75% on ETH when re-run elsewhere ([FineFT](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/qin-2025-fineft-ensemble-rl-crypto-futures.md)).
- Seed dispersion is as large as the effect: one configuration gave CAGR from -3% to +199% across five seeds ([Lebiedz and Slepaczuk 2026](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/lebiedz-2026-drl-execution-overlay-crypto-pair-trading.md)).
- Standard environments overstate performance (bar-close fills, no spread, no self-impact), and only one environment read models margin, funding and liquidation ([topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)). Offline and model-based RL do not solve the single-history problem.
- RL on perp-state inputs (funding, premium) lost money out of sample in the one study with a chronological test and costs ([Wang et al. 2026](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/notes/wang-2026-m3ft-multi-agent-perpetual-futures-rl.md)). No RL study uses macro events intraday, and about two hundred events is too few for end-to-end learning ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- A direction-only objective on Kalshi will learn to overtrade; the choice between resting and crossing should be part of the policy ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)). A replay backtest cannot answer the execution question on a thin book; it needs live exploration data ([topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md), open question 9).

### 5.10 Where evolutionary search fits, and where the notes warn against it

Fits:

- **Parameter and rule search inside a fixed protocol**: three windows (search, select, test once), costs inside the fitness function and set above the real cost, complexity caps, stationary inputs, and a language that cannot express look-ahead ([Neely et al. 1997](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/neely-1997-genetic-programming-fx-technical-analysis.md), [Allen and Karjalainen 1999](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/allen-1999-genetic-algorithms-technical-trading-rules.md), [topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- **Ensembles, not champions.** The rule ranked first in the selection period was among the four worst of 100 out of sample, while a majority vote of all 100 was profitable in every currency ([Neely et al. 1997](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/neely-1997-genetic-programming-fx-technical-analysis.md)).
- **Inertia across refits.** Changing position only when a supermajority of recent rules agrees roughly doubled net return against a static rule ([Martin et al. 2019](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/martin-2019-grammatical-evolution-ensembles-algorithmic-trading.md), daily equities).
- **Fitness built on a null.** Real-minus-placebo difference for level definitions ([topic 17](../topics/17-auction-orderflow-practitioner/SYNTHESIS.md)), a permutation-test quantile for sparse event signals ([topic 15](../topics/15-demark-indicators/SYNTHESIS.md)), and a residual test against simple known predictors ([Wang and Ventre 2026](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/wang-2026-factorbench-automated-factor-mining-benchmark.md)).
- **Search over execution and sizing logic around a fixed forecaster** is proposed as a safer target; the one study is a proof of concept on generous fills ([Kvasiuk et al. 2026](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/kvasiuk-2026-madevolve-llm-evolution-bitcoin-trading.md), grade C).

Warnings:

- Search finds in-sample structure easily and most of it does not survive costs out of sample. At half-hour frequency in currencies, genetic programming found nothing after realistic costs, and a fitted autoregression did at least as well ([Neely and Weller 2003](../topics/11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/notes/neely-2003-intraday-technical-trading-fx-genetic.md)).
- Turnover is where evolved rules die: left alone, search selects rules that trade often ([topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md)). A crypto post-mortem reports models that predicted 5 bp moves against an 8 bp round-trip fee ([Chen 2025](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/chen-2025-red-queen-deep-evolution-crypto-hft.md), grade D).
- Compare with chance at equal budget. Genetic programming beat random search given the same number of candidates in 3 of 9 markets ([Chen and Navet 2007](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/notes/chen-2007-genetic-programming-versus-random-search-pretests.md)). Buy-and-hold is not an adequate null.
- Count every trial. No LLM-discovered strategy could be distinguished from luck once every trial was counted ([Gencay 2026](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/gencay-2026-honest-evaluation-llm-strategy-discovery.md)); a Bayesian tuner on BTC perpetuals left a probability of backtest overfitting of 0.586 ([Deng 2025](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/deng-2025-autoquant-execution-constrained-crypto-perps.md)).
- Populations collapse onto one idea unless diversity is enforced ([topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Do not let a search condition freely on the clock or the announcement calendar; it will find calendar rules because a few large event days dominate returns ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 10](../topics/10-intraday-seasonality-crypto/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Kalshi's five months of history contain several fee and leverage regimes; a search over it will fit regime artefacts unless each regime is held out in turn ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Evolution strategies need 3 to 10 times more data than gradient methods, and a market offers one history; neuroevolution has no trading evidence in the repository ([topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)).

### 5.11 Governance for all three

- Keep a ledger of every backtest and deflate the winner by the effective trial count ([Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/bailey-2014-deflated-sharpe-ratio.md), [Arnott et al. 2019](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/arnott-2019-backtesting-protocol-machine-learning.md)). Five years of data support only about 45 independent trials before a Sharpe of 1 is expected by luck ([Bailey et al. 2014](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/notes/bailey-2014-pseudo-mathematics-financial-charlatanism.md)).
- Expect to keep about half of a backtested edge ([topic 26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Measure costs from the bot's own fills before trusting any backtest ([topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- A sensible order of work, following the open questions the topics rank first: record Kalshi data and measure markout and fill rates with minimum-size orders; build the seasonal, volatility and liquidity models; run the shock event study and the horizon map on SOL; trade an equal-weight blend through a band; only then admit learned challengers, each against the standing baselines.

## 7. Main gaps that remain

**The venue**

- Nothing is measured on Kalshi beyond one day: no lag or information share against Binance or the index, no basis half-life, no markout or fill rate for resting orders, no behaviour during an offshore cascade or around releases, no spread and depth profile by hour ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Unknown contract mechanics: true liquidation distance, loss allocation if liquidations fail, the fee a retail account actually pays, and which funding average is applied ([topic 06](../topics/06-kalshi-venue/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 04](../topics/04-open-interest-liquidations/SYNTHESIS.md)).

**The asset**

- SOL-specific evidence is nearly absent. Where SOL appears it is one asset among several: in the Hyperliquid funding, liquidity and deleveraging studies, the liquidity-measure volatility classifier, the 29-asset lead-lag study, and the quarter-hour study, where it is the weakest of six. The syntheses on regimes, macro response, cross-asset lead-lag, intraday seasonality, volatility forecasting, impact and order-book signals each report no SOL-specific test.
- Whether Solana DEX prices, tips, priority fees or failure rates carry any lead for SOL is untested ([topic 19](../topics/19-onchain-flows-solana/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 05](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/SYNTHESIS.md)).

**The core directional question**

- No event study of signed returns after many liquidation bursts, net of the spreads that prevail in those minutes; no validation of liquidation-map levels; no signed-return study around funding timestamps ([topic 28](../topics/28-shock-response-price-pressure/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- No out-of-sample study of funding, premium, open interest or long/short ratios as predictors of the perp's own return at minutes to hours, and no learned model that ablates them ([topic 03](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 23](../topics/23-ml-feature-prediction/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- The horizon at which reversal gives way to continuation has not been located for any crypto asset ([topic 13](../topics/13-momentum-reversal/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 16](../topics/16-oscillators-technical-rules/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Most crypto evidence on overreaction, seasonality, technical rules and on-chain flows ends between 2017 and 2023 and uses spot data.

**Transmission**

- No measured lead from equity index futures to BTC or SOL at seconds to minutes ([topic 30](../topics/30-macro-state-intraday-transmission/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- The most important macro source, the Federal Reserve staff study of crypto responses to news, is held only as a secondary note ([Kroner et al. 2026](../topics/02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/kroner-2026-cryptocurrencies-price-economic-news.md)).
- Sub-minute lead-lag between BTC and SOL, and the sign of cross-coin predictability, rest on abstract-only notes or are missing ([topic 21](../topics/21-cross-asset-lead-lag/SYNTHESIS.md)).

**Volatility, cost and risk**

- HAR-type forecasting at 15 minutes to 4 hours on crypto perpetuals is essentially untested ([topic 14](../topics/14-volatility-forecasting/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- No source combines Kelly sizing, a volatility forecast, funding costs and a liquidation boundary.
- No study of stop-losses in intraday crypto, of latency cost for a non-HFT participant on a crypto venue, or of exchange and operational risk ([topic 27](../topics/27-execution-risk-management/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- Survivorship bias, strategy capacity and decay rates of intraday crypto signals are not covered ([topic 26](../topics/26-backtest-overfitting/SYNTHESIS.md)).

**Learning methods**

- No clean head-to-head of RL against a supervised forecast plus a rule on intraday crypto with matched features, costs and seeds ([topic 24](../topics/24-reinforcement-learning/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- No comparison of evolutionary against Bayesian search at equal budget, no credible neuroevolution or grammatical-evolution study on crypto, and no study that combines perpetual futures, realistic fills and trial accounting ([topic 25](../topics/25-evolutionary-algorithms/SYNTHESIS.md), [topic 31](../topics/31-rl-evolutionary-second-pass/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- No test of signal combination, bandit selection or drift detection at intraday frequency net of fees ([topic 29](../topics/29-signal-combination-online-adaptation/SYNTHESIS.md)).
- No test of a model trained on a large venue and applied to a small one, which is the design this bot depends on ([topic 22](../topics/22-deep-learning-lob/SYNTHESIS.md)).

**The repository itself**

- Most topics report that the web-search budget ran out part-way, so coverage of 2025 and 2026 work is partial, and each synthesis lists works that were sought and not obtained.
- Fifty notes rest on abstracts and six on secondary descriptions, including several that carry weight above: crypto carry and crash risk ([Schmeling et al. 2023](../topics/03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/schmeling-2023-crypto-carry.md)), offshore price discovery ([Alexander and Heck 2020](../topics/05-price-discovery-cross-venue/notes/alexander-2020-price-discovery-bitcoin-unregulated-markets.md)), and both sides of the cross-coin predictability dispute.
- The same work is noted under more than one topic with different slugs and sometimes different grades.
- `INDEX.md` and `VALIDATION.md` were regenerated on 2026-11-22 after the last notes were written and cover all 530 notes and 31 syntheses. Rerun `tools/build_index.py` after adding or editing notes.
