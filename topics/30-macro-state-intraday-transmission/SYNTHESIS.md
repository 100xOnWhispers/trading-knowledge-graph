# Macro state variables and intraday cross-market transmission into crypto: synthesis

Coverage note: the session's shared web-search budget was exhausted for most of the run, so discovery relied on a few searches plus known identifiers and reference lists. The announcement canon and the state-measure papers were read in full. The crypto-specific intraday literature is thin and the single most important source, Kroner, Mohammed and Vega (2026), could not be opened in full. Gaps are listed at the end of the second section.

## What the literature agrees on

- **The surprise, not the release, moves prices, and the measurement template is settled.** Standardised surprise z = (actual minus consensus) / historical standard deviation; regress the window return on z using announcement observations only ([Andersen et al. 2002](notes/andersen-2002-micro-effects-macro-announcements-fx.md)). Every later paper in this topic uses it, including the crypto work known through topic 02.
- **The mean response is fast and the volatility response is slow.** In FX and futures the price adjusts within the first five-minute bar while volatility stays elevated for about an hour ([Andersen et al. 2002](notes/andersen-2002-micro-effects-macro-announcements-fx.md), [Andersen et al. 2005](notes/andersen-2005-real-time-price-discovery-stock-bond-fx.md)). Around FOMC announcements the excess variance of equity futures and exchange rates lasts ten hours or more ([Boehm and Kroner 2025](notes/boehm-2025-fed-non-yield-shock.md)).
- **US news is the global risk impulse.** US macro surprises move stock indices of 27 countries, the VIX and commodities within 30 minutes, by about as much as the S&P 500; foreign releases barely move US equities ([Boehm and Kroner 2023](notes/boehm-2023-us-economic-news-global-financial-cycle.md)). US monetary policy drives a single global factor in risky assets ([Miranda-Agrippino and Rey 2019](notes/miranda-agrippino-2019-us-monetary-policy-global-financial-cycle.md)). Crypto reacting to US releases with the sign of the equity response is what this channel predicts.
- **Response coefficients are state dependent.** The equity response to growth news flips sign between expansion and recession ([Andersen et al. 2005](notes/andersen-2005-real-time-price-discovery-stock-bond-fx.md)). The response to CPI rose by an order of magnitude in 2021 to 2023 and scales with pre-release attention; Bitcoin's 60-minute response to a one-standard-deviation CPI surprise went from about -4 basis points (insignificant) to about -114 ([Kroner 2025](notes/kroner-2025-macro-news-investor-attention.md)). A fixed beta per release is wrong in sign or size much of the time.
- **Not all releases matter, and timing in the data cycle explains which.** Early releases in each information category carry the impact; late, accurate ones do not ([Andersen et al. 2002](notes/andersen-2002-micro-effects-macro-announcements-fx.md), [Gilbert et al. 2015](notes/gilbert-2015-intrinsic-value-macro-announcements.md)).
- **FOMC news has more than one dimension.** Target and path factors ([Gurkaynak, Sack and Swanson 2004](notes/gurkaynak-2004-actions-words-target-path-factors.md)); a component orthogonal to all yields that moves equities and the dollar through risk premia ([Boehm and Kroner 2025](notes/boehm-2025-fed-non-yield-shock.md)); and Chair speeches and press conferences that matter as much as statements ([Bauer and Swanson 2022](notes/bauer-2022-reassessment-monetary-policy-surprises.md)).
- **Prices move before scheduled news.** Equity and Treasury futures drift in the direction of the surprise from about 30 minutes before some releases ([Kurov et al. 2016](notes/kurov-2016-price-drift-before-macro-news.md)); equity futures earn a premium overnight before payrolls, ISM, GDP and FOMC, tied to a build-up and release of implied volatility ([Hu et al. 2021](notes/hu-2021-premium-heightened-uncertainty-pre-announcement.md)).
- **Risk appetite is measurable daily from market prices, and it is not the same as uncertainty.** A first principal component of credit, equity-volatility, funding and currency changes ([Chari et al. 2023](notes/chari-2023-risk-on-risk-off-multifaceted.md)); the variance risk premium for risk aversion and credit spreads for uncertainty ([Bekaert et al. 2019](notes/bekaert-2019-time-variation-risk-appetite-uncertainty.md)); a weekly broad financial conditions index ([Brave and Butters 2011](notes/brave-2011-monitoring-financial-stability-fci.md)).
- **Lagged macro variables are poor directional predictors of crypto.** In a 141-predictor audit of daily Bitcoin returns, 5.6 percent of macro exercises survive out of sample against 32.2 percent of technical ones ([Sanhueza 2026](notes/sanhueza-2026-bitcoin-return-predictability-comprehensive.md)). Machine-learning studies in neighbouring topics find the same ordering ([Dudek et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/dudek-2025-bitcoin-volatility-gradient-boosting.md), [Amirzadeh et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/amirzadeh-2023-causal-reinforcement-learning-crypto-trading.md)). Macro matters as a contemporaneous driver, an event schedule and a regime descriptor.

## Where it disagrees or is weak

- **Speed of adjustment: jump or drift.** The canon finds a jump inside five minutes. Crypto evidence in topic 02 (Kroner, Mohammed and Vega, secondary access) reports adjustment over 15 to 30 minutes, and [Kroner 2025](notes/kroner-2025-macro-news-investor-attention.md) uses 60-minute windows because some instruments need longer. Whether the slower crypto adjustment is real underreaction or an artefact of window choice is unresolved, and it decides whether a non-colocated bot has anything to trade.
- **Pre-announcement drift: informed trading, risk premium, or gone.** [Kurov et al. 2016](notes/kurov-2016-price-drift-before-macro-news.md) attribute drift to leakage and proprietary data, and find it only after 2008 and only for seven releases, not CPI or payrolls. [Hu et al. 2021](notes/hu-2021-premium-heightened-uncertainty-pre-announcement.md) explain a different, overnight drift as an uncertainty premium. Topic 11 holds notes showing the pre-FOMC drift weakened after publication ([Lucca and Moench 2018](../11-intraday-seasonality-tradfi/notes/lucca-2018-pre-fomc-drift-recent-evidence.md)). The topic 02 abstract-only note on [Ben Omrane et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/benomrane-2025-crypto-volatility-intraday-macro-news.md) says much of the crypto volatility response precedes the release. None of this has been tested for direction in crypto.
- **No measured lead from equity futures to crypto.** This was the question topic 21 left open, and it stays open. Targeted searches found no study estimating lead-lag between ES or NQ and BTC, ETH or SOL at seconds to minutes. What exists is contemporaneous: a persistent positive 30-minute beta in US hours since late 2020 ([Drexler et al.](../21-cross-asset-lead-lag/notes/drexler-2026-bitcoin-rising-beta-equities.md)), and strong contemporaneous cross-market links among stock, bond and FX futures identified through heteroskedasticity ([Andersen et al. 2005](notes/andersen-2005-real-time-price-discovery-stock-bond-fx.md)). A Southampton thesis (Long, 2025) was seen only in a search summary: Bitcoin often lagging other coins at tick level, with lead-lag effects weakening around the US equity open. The equity-futures-as-surprise-proxy idea rests on inference.
- **Which state measure.** The risk-on/risk-off index, the variance premium, the global factor and financial conditions indices overlap heavily, and [Chari et al.](notes/chari-2023-risk-on-risk-off-multifaceted.md) argue the VIX alone has weakened as a proxy since 2008. No source tests any of them as a conditioning variable for crypto at intraday horizons. All are contemporaneous summaries of prices crypto already reacts to.
- **Liquidity leads crypto: a belief without a test.** The practitioner claim is a 13-week lead with a beta of 7.5 to 9, from correlations on smoothed overlapping changes ([Howell 2026](notes/howell-2026-bitcoin-liquidity-trusted-untrusted.md)). The machine-learning version finds a liquidity input helps only beyond a month and on one 120-day test window ([Karthick et al. 2026](notes/karthick-2026-bitcoin-forecasting-global-liquidity-timexer.md)). Topic 02 holds evidence against a fixed M2 lead ([Molnar et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/molnar-2026-bitcoin-halving-clock.md)). No source was found that tests central-bank net liquidity (balance sheet minus Treasury account minus reverse repo) or stablecoin supply against crypto with sign-stability and out-of-sample checks. Dollar-converted aggregates also embed the dollar index, so any test needs a dollar-only benchmark.
- **Predictable policy surprises.** [Bauer and Swanson](notes/bauer-2022-reassessment-monetary-policy-surprises.md) find 16 percent of the FOMC surprise is explained ex post by public data, and argue it was not exploitable ex ante. Whether a pre-meeting lean has value is open.
- **Machine learning, reinforcement learning and evolutionary methods: almost no evidence in scope.** No study was found that gives an RL agent, a gradient-boosted model, a genetic-programming search or a language-model agent macro state or event features at intraday frequency and reports an ablation. The nearest items are: an RL perpetual-futures trader whose language-model market-state layer helped while rule-based state layers did not, on daily bars with seed dispersion larger than the mean ([Wang et al. 2026](notes/wang-2026-m3ft-micro-macro-micro-perpetual-futures.md)); feature-group selection that rejected macro inputs for four of five coins ([Amirzadeh et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/amirzadeh-2023-causal-reinforcement-learning-crypto-trading.md)); regime-specialised sub-agents on one-minute crypto with regimes defined from price trend and volatility, not macro ([Zong et al.](../24-reinforcement-learning/notes/zong-2024-macrohft-memory-context-rl-crypto.md)); a language-model hawk-dove index with small, mostly insignificant gains at weekly horizons ([Nicolas et al.](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/nicolas-2026-bitcoin-monetary-policy-expectations-llm.md)). The absence is itself a finding: the common belief that macro features help learned crypto traders is untested at the horizon this bot trades. With roughly 8 FOMC, 12 CPI and 12 payroll events a year, a learned policy sees on the order of two hundred headline macro events in the whole post-2021 sample, which is far too few for end-to-end learning and argues for hand-specified event features with estimated coefficients.
- **Samples.** The canon is pre-2020 and often pre-2008. Crypto results exist only for the post-2021 regime, which contains one inflation episode. SOL appears in no intraday macro study read here; it enters only as 10 percent of a practitioner basket.
- **Not covered or not accessible.** Kroner, Mohammed and Vega (2026), "How Do Cryptocurrencies Price Economic News?": SSRN refused access, and the paper is not on the first author's page, his public file repository or the Federal Reserve working paper indexes; a search summary of a third-party digest adds that the data are from CoinDesk for 2015 to 2025 covering Coinbase, Binance and OKX, and that bid-ask spreads widen around releases (unverified). Ben Omrane et al. (2025): publisher blocked. Long (2025, Southampton thesis): repository bot protection. IMF papers (Che et al. WP 23/163; Iyer 2022; Iyer and Popescu WP 23/213): downloads refused, and they are daily studies. Not located for lack of search budget: Pyo and Lee (2020); Mourey et al. (2025); Karau (journal version); Hasbrouck (2003); Kurov, Wolfe and Gilbert (2021); Savor and Wilson (2013); Cieslak, Morse and Vissing-Jorgensen (2019); Bernile, Hu and Tang (2016); Kritzman, Page and Turkington (2012); current Chicago Fed NFCI documentation; Howell's book on the liquidity construction; equity and rates volatility term-structure measures as regime gates; BTC dominance as a state variable; Federal Reserve, BIS and New York Fed notes on crypto and macro (the Federal Reserve note indexes for 2024 to 2026 were scanned and list only stablecoin and ETP titles). A CME Group research piece on Bitcoin's correlation with equities was seen in a search summary and not opened.

Notes for learned models (reinforcement learning, supervised learning, evolutionary search), drawn from the sources above and not from any direct test:

- Macro inputs suit three roles: event features (time to next release, time since last, standardised surprise once known, release category), state features (risk index, variance premium, attention) and reward or risk shaping (position limits and volatility scaling in the post-release window). They do not suit the role of raw predictors, on the evidence of the out-of-sample audit.
- Event counts are small. Coefficients on event features should be estimated by pooled regression across BTC, ETH and SOL and across release types, then supplied to a policy as fixed or slowly updated inputs.
- An evolutionary or language-model strategy search that is allowed to condition on the announcement calendar will find calendar rules easily, because a few large event days dominate returns. The search-count deflation and look-ahead controls described in topic 25 ([Gencay et al.](../25-evolutionary-algorithms/notes/gencay-2026-honest-evaluation-llm-strategy-discovery.md)) apply with extra force, and point-in-time consensus data is required so that the surprise is not computed from revised figures.
- Any ablation should report the model with and without macro features on event windows and non-event windows separately, across seeds, with the standard deviation shown, since the one RL ablation read here had dispersion larger than its effect.

## Open questions worth testing on SOL perps

1. Lead-lag from ES, NQ, ZN and dollar-index futures to BTC, ETH and SOL perpetuals at 1 second to 5 minutes, separately for the 30 minutes after 8:30 and 10:00 ET releases, the 9:30 open, the 16:00 close, FOMC statement and press conference, and ordinary US hours. Use the Hayashi-Yoshida and Hawkes tools noted in topic 21. Does any lead survive the Kalshi spread?
2. Cumulative SOL response to CPI, payrolls and FOMC at 10 seconds, 1, 5, 15, 30, 60 minutes and 6 hours, measured from a t - 30 minute anchor, year by year from 2021. Is the 15-to-30-minute adjustment reported for BTC and ETH present, and has it shortened?
3. Does the first-minute ES and VIX futures move predict the next 5 to 60 minutes of SOL better than the numeric surprise? Does it remove the need for a regime-dependent sign on growth news?
4. Does SOL load on the FOMC non-yield residual, and does the drift continue through the press conference? How long does SOL's excess variance last after 14:00 ET on FOMC days?
5. Attention scaling: do rolling news-count or pre-release volume measures predict the absolute size of the SOL response per release type? Has the CPI beta fallen since 2023 while a labour-data beta rose?
6. Pre-release windows: is there signed drift in SOL in the 30 minutes before the Kurov drift-list releases, or overnight before payrolls and FOMC conditional on a DVOL or VIX build-up?
7. Regime gating: do SOL's intraday beta to ES, the profitability of momentum and reversal signals, or funding dynamics differ across terciles of a daily risk-on/risk-off index and of the variance premium? Test with thresholds fixed in a training period.
8. Net liquidity and stablecoin supply: with the lag fixed in advance, rolling-window sign stability, overlapping-observation corrections and a dollar-index-only benchmark, does any liquidity series add out-of-sample information for weekly SOL or BTC returns?
9. BTC dominance and SOL/BTC as state variables: does the response of SOL to macro surprises, relative to BTC, depend on dominance or its trend?
10. Learned models: train the same supervised or RL model with (a) no macro inputs, (b) event features only, (c) event plus state features; evaluate on event and non-event windows across at least ten seeds with a search-count-deflated statistic. Does (b) or (c) beat (a) anywhere other than risk control?
11. Kalshi-specific: how do spread and depth on the Kalshi SOL perpetual behave in the minute before and five minutes after releases relative to Binance, and does the 8-hour funding window interact with 8:30 ET releases?

## Reading order

2. [Andersen, Bollerslev, Diebold and Vega 2005](notes/andersen-2005-real-time-price-discovery-stock-bond-fx.md): state-dependent sign in equities and cross-market links.
3. [Boehm and Kroner 2023](notes/boehm-2023-us-economic-news-global-financial-cycle.md): US news as the global risk impulse; the channel that reaches crypto.
4. [Kroner 2025](notes/kroner-2025-macro-news-investor-attention.md): attention scaling and the only full-text intraday Bitcoin response in this topic.
5. [Gurkaynak, Sack and Swanson 2004](notes/gurkaynak-2004-actions-words-target-path-factors.md), then [Bauer and Swanson 2022](notes/bauer-2022-reassessment-monetary-policy-surprises.md) and [Boehm and Kroner 2025](notes/boehm-2025-fed-non-yield-shock.md): how FOMC surprises are built and what they miss.
