# Topic 19: On-chain flows, ETF and stablecoin flows, and Solana-specific drivers

Twenty-two source notes. Fourteen are PDFs read in full or in the relevant parts, four are web pages (industry reports and documentation) read in full, three were read from the abstract only, and one through a third-party summary. Web search was rate-limited for most of the session, so coverage leans on arXiv, NBER and pages that could be fetched directly; the gaps are listed at the end of the open-questions section.

The short version: flow data carry some short-lived information, the published effects are small, almost none of them were tested on SOL, and the Solana-specific series that are fast enough to matter at minutes to hours (priority fees, tips, failure rates, DEX activity) have never been tested against SOL returns in anything read here.

## What the literature agrees on

1. **Raw on-chain data are mostly noise until entities are labelled.** About 90 percent of Bitcoin on-chain volume is not economically meaningful, and three quarters of the rest involves exchanges ([makarov-2021](notes/makarov-2021-blockchain-analysis-bitcoin-market.md)). On Solana more than half of non-vote transactions failed over a full year and bots fail 58 percent of the time ([zheng-2025](notes/zheng-2025-failed-transactions-solana-blockchain.md)); 28 million sandwich attacks add non-directional volume to DEX prints ([heimbach-2026](notes/heimbach-2026-protected-order-flow-sandwich-attacks.md)). Every flow signal is a statement about a vendor's address labels and filters as much as about the market.

2. **Deposits to exchanges carry more information than withdrawals.** Coin inflows to exchanges are associated with lower subsequent returns at daily ([hoang-2021](notes/hoang-2021-bitcoin-exchange-reserves-returns-volatility.md)) and hourly ([chi-2024](notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md), for ETH) horizons; outflows show no consistent relationship. The stated reason is that deposits mostly precede selling while withdrawals have many motives.

3. **Flow effects are short-lived.** Bitcoin's response to a Tether mint peaks at 30 minutes and fades by 60 ([saggu-2022](notes/saggu-2022-intraday-bitcoin-tether-minting-burning.md)). Large-transfer effects are confined to minutes around confirmation ([ante-2020](notes/ante-2020-market-reaction-large-bitcoin-transfers.md)). USDT exchange inflows predict returns at 1 and 2 hours but not at 3 to 6 ([chi-2024](notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md)). Daily exchange-flow effects are gone after one lag ([ciaian-2026](notes/ciaian-2026-onchain-offchain-drivers-bitcoin-price.md)). This is why daily studies often find nothing, and it is the right horizon for this repository.

4. **Price drives flows at least as much as flows drive price.** Price drops are followed by exchange inflows the next day ([hoang-2021](notes/hoang-2021-bitcoin-exchange-reserves-returns-volatility.md)); daily ETF flows are explained mainly by same-day price changes ([oefele-2025](notes/oefele-2025-one-year-bitcoin-spot-etps-fund-flows.md)). Any flow feature must be tested with lagged returns as controls.

5. **Effect sizes are small relative to costs.** A 1 billion USDT mint moves Bitcoin 0.68 percent at the peak; large-transfer abnormal returns are hundredths of a percent. Flow features are plausible as conditioning inputs to a model, not as stand-alone entry rules.

6. **Solana's on-chain activity is concentrated and regime-dependent.** In late 2024 one launchpad produced up to 71 percent of new tokens and 40 to 67 percent of DEX transactions ([mancino-2025](notes/mancino-2025-memecoin-phenomenon-solana-pumpfun.md)); ecosystem series and the SOL price peaked together ([xiao-2026](notes/xiao-2026-solana-digital-asset-forecasting-dataset.md)); by mid-2026 network revenue had fallen 43 percent in a quarter as that activity unwound ([blockworks-2026](notes/blockworks-2026-solana-tokenholder-report-q2.md)).

## Where it disagrees or is weak

- **Does stablecoin issuance move prices?** [griffin-2020](notes/griffin-2020-is-bitcoin-really-untethered.md) says yes, conditionally, after downturns in 2017. [lyons-2020](notes/lyons-2020-what-keeps-stablecoins-stable.md) finds no systematic daily effect of aggregate issuance. [saggu-2022](notes/saggu-2022-intraday-bitcoin-tether-minting-burning.md) reconciles them in part: there is an effect, it lasts under an hour, it applies to mints and not burns, and it depends on sentiment and on public announcement, which makes it look like an attention effect. None of the three covers the market after 2021.
- **Sign and strength differ by asset.** At hourly horizons coin inflows are bearish for ETH but have no clear effect for BTC, where the one significant coefficient is positive ([chi-2024](notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md)). Hot-wallet deposits were followed by price rises in [ante-2020](notes/ante-2020-market-reaction-large-bitcoin-transfers.md), against the folklore. Nothing can be assumed for SOL.
- **ETF flows.** The claim that ETF flows drive price rests largely on levels regressions in which the regressor contains the price ([guliyev-2025](notes/guliyev-2025-cointegration-bitcoin-spot-etf-assets.md)). The better-identified abstract says flows follow price ([oefele-2025](notes/oefele-2025-one-year-bitcoin-spot-etps-fund-flows.md)). A 2026 preprint opened in this session (Ebadollahyvahed and Umeorah, arXiv:2610.35702, on weekend crypto volatility) reports that interactions with ETF flow activity gave no evidence of amplification. For SOL the flows are small: USD 120 million of spot ETP inflow in a quarter, and treasury-company holdings flat for nine months ([blockworks-2026](notes/blockworks-2026-solana-tokenholder-report-q2.md)).
- **Token unlocks.** The only evidence is one market maker's report with an undisclosed sample period and no statistical tests ([keyrock-2024](notes/keyrock-2024-token-unlocks-price-impact.md)). Its horizon is weeks.
- **Solana outages, staking and MEV versus price.** There is no rigorous study of any of these against SOL returns in what was found. The outage record is seven events ([helius-2025-outages](notes/helius-2025-solana-outages-complete-history.md)); staking mechanics are documented but untested as a signal ([anza-2026](notes/anza-2026-stake-delegation-warmup-cooldown.md)); MEV and tip data are descriptive ([helius-2025-mev](notes/helius-2025-solana-mev-report.md)).
- **Machine learning, reinforcement learning and evolutionary methods on on-chain data.** The work exists but is weak for this purpose. It is daily or coarser, centred on Bitcoin, and evaluated loosely: a reinforcement-learning agent with on-chain inputs tested on one bear-market window against long-only baselines with no ablation and no costs ([huang-2023](notes/huang-2023-reinforcement-learning-onchain-portfolio-management.md)); a feature-importance study that predicts price levels and tunes with k-fold cross-validation ([demosthenous-2024](notes/demosthenous-2024-onchain-macro-data-source-diversity.md)); a deep-learning study with genetic-algorithm feature selection that reports 82 percent directional accuracy and a 6,654 percent annual return, which is not credible without replication ([omole-2024](notes/omole-2024-deep-learning-bitcoin-direction-onchain.md)). An LLM-agent paper that feeds on-chain transaction statistics for BTC, ETH and SOL into daily trading decisions (Li et al., "CryptoTrade", arXiv:2407.09546) was opened but not noted; by its own account its performance is comparable to buy-and-hold and MACD and it is not always the top performer. The transferable content is procedural: per-asset feature selection, several importance measures, selection inside the walk-forward loop.
- **Magnitudes.** Several working papers report effect sizes that cannot be right as printed ([chi-2024](notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md)); rely on signs and horizons.
- **Decay.** The cleanest intraday results use data ending in 2019 to 2023. Whale-alert bots, exchange-flow dashboards and mint trackers are now ubiquitous, so the public versions of these signals have probably weakened. No paper read here tests post-2023 data.

## Open questions worth testing on SOL perps

1. **Do the hourly flow regressions replicate on SOL, and at what bar size?** Run the [chi-2024](notes/chi-2024-return-volatility-forecasting-onchain-flows.md) specification for SOL exchange net inflow and for USDT plus USDC net inflow at 5, 15 and 60 minutes, with lagged returns as controls, on post-2023 data. Solana's finality is sub-second, so if deposits predict selling the lag should be much shorter than for ETH.
2. **Is there any SOL-specific information in Solana stablecoin mints**, or does SOL just take its beta share of a market-wide response? Repeat the [saggu-2022](notes/saggu-2022-intraday-bitcoin-tether-minting-burning.md) event study with USDC and USDT mints on Solana versus mints on other chains.
3. **Do tips, priority fees and price-related failure rates lead SOL realised volatility or signed returns** by minutes, or do they lag centralised-exchange price? This is the most promising untested Solana-native input because it updates every slot.
4. **Does the deactivating-stake queue predict exchange inflows or returns around epoch boundaries?** Label the owners of large deactivations (exchanges, ETF custodians, treasury companies, liquid-staking pools) and test the hours after the boundary.
5. **What does SOL do in the first hour of a halt or severe degradation**, and how does the perp basis on centralised venues and on Kalshi behave when deposits and withdrawals stop? Seven historical events is too few for statistics, so this is a scenario and risk-rule question.
6. **Is there anything left in SOL ETF flow after removing the return-chasing component?** Test next-session returns on the flow surprise once enough history exists (US spot SOL ETFs date from October 2025).
7. **Does cleaned Solana DEX signed flow** (failed transactions and sandwich legs removed, proprietary-AMM volume separated) add information beyond centralised-exchange order flow for SOL? Topic 05 covers price discovery; the DEX side is the piece that belongs here.
8. **Label stability.** How much do vendor exchange-flow series for SOL change when re-downloaded a month later? If revisions are material, every backtest in this topic is suspect.
9. **Can a learned model use these features at all?** Before any reinforcement-learning or evolutionary search, check whether a plain regularised regression on the candidate features beats a returns-only baseline out of sample after costs. The published learning papers skipped this step.

Sub-areas not covered because sources could not be found or opened: research from Glassnode, CryptoQuant, Kaiko and Chainalysis (no page fetched); Galaxy research on Solana; peer-reviewed work on ETF flows by Mazur and Polyzos and by Shalvardjiev (seen in search results only); the measurement paper on Jito sandwiching by Gerzon and co-authors at IMC 2025 (index entry only); on-chain perp venues (Drift and Jupiter documentation pages did not return usable content); any event study of SOL around the release of locked or estate-held SOL; and a Blockworks news analysis of SOL price around outages (HTTP 403).
