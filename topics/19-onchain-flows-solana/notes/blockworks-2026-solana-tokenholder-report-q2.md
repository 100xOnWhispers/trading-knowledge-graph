## Key insight
The most recent quantified picture of the SOL-specific flow channels. In the second quarter of 2026, SOL spot ETP net inflows were USD 120 million, treasury companies held a flat 16.8 million SOL, and 427 million SOL (about two thirds of supply) was staked. Against those, staker rewards, almost all new issuance, were USD 487 million. Network revenue fell 43 percent as memecoin activity unwound. On these numbers, issuance is the largest recurring SOL flow, and ETF and treasury demand are small beside it.

## Method
Descriptive quarterly statistics from Blockworks' own data platform, organised into financials, institutional flows, sector analysis and network analysis. No statistical tests and no analysis of price effects. Roughly the first 30 pages were read.

## Data and sample
Solana mainnet and SOL investment products, April to June 2026, with quarter-on-quarter comparisons.

## Reported results
- ETPs: SOL spot ETP net inflows USD 120 million (USD 113 million in the first quarter); all SOL ETPs USD 148 million, made up of USD 185 million into US products and USD 38 million out of European ones. BTC spot ETPs lost USD 3.7 billion and ETH USD 0.5 billion in the same quarter. SOL spot ETP assets ended at USD 1.9 billion. US spot SOL ETFs began trading in October 2025.
- Treasury companies: 16.8 million SOL, down 0.9 percent, unchanged for a ninth month; aggregate NAV USD 1.2 billion; described as constrained by discounts to NAV and as "a stable, passive holder base rather than a source of incremental demand".
- Staking: 427 million SOL staked. Nominal yield about 5.5 percent, inflation about 3.8 percent. Stakers earned USD 487 million, over 98 percent from issuance.
- Real Economic Value (fees plus tips): USD 51.0 million, down 43 percent; priority fees USD 30.8 million, Jito tips USD 9.9 million.
- DEX spot volume: USD 160.8 billion, down 44 percent, still 32 percent of all-chain DEX volume. SOL-stablecoin pairs about 46 percent of volume, memecoins about 17 percent. Proprietary AMMs handled 53 percent of spot DEX volume.
- Perps: about USD 183 billion notional on Solana venues, up 60 percent; GMTrade 50 percent, Pacifica 39 percent, Jupiter 10 percent. Drift suffered an exploit on 1 April 2026 affecting roughly half its TVL.
- Launchpads: USD 25.8 billion volume, 2.6 million tokens launched; Pump.fun earned 97 percent of launchpad revenue.
- Network: 9.8 billion non-vote transactions, 27 percent reverted; median fee USD 0.0004; stablecoin supply USD 16.3 billion.
- Proposals: SIMD-550 would double the disinflation rate; SIMD-553 would add a usage-linked burn estimated at 7,500 to 9,000 SOL per day.

## Signal construction
Not a signal. It sizes the candidate inputs so effort goes to the ones large enough to matter:
- Daily SOL ETP net flow (order of USD 2 million a day on average) is tiny beside SOL exchange volume.
- Treasury-company holdings: no flow to model while holdings are flat; watch for forced selling if discounts persist.
- Staking and unstaking queue changes at epoch boundaries and the issuance stream are the large recurring supply flows.
- DEX volume, priority fees and tips are the fast activity series (see [helius-2025-solana-mev-report](helius-2025-solana-mev-report.md)).

## Relevance to SOL perpetuals at minutes to hours
Sets priors. ETF and treasury flows are too small and too slow to be a primary intraday driver in 2026, although announcements can still move price as news. The decline in memecoin-driven revenue means relationships between on-chain activity and SOL estimated on 2024 to early 2025 data may not hold. The shift of on-chain perp volume away from Drift and Jupiter means older studies of those venues describe a market that has moved.

## Caveats
Commissioned and funded by the Solana Foundation, which may have input on content; the tone is promotional. Proprietary data that cannot be reproduced. One quarter. No evidence on whether any of these flows predicts SOL returns.
