# Topic 18: Trader positioning, sentiment and attention

Scope: signals from who is positioned how (exchange long/short ratios, retail versus large traders, CFTC reports, Hyperliquid wallet data), social and news sentiment including LLM-scored text, fear-and-greed composites, and search or attention measures, with their predictive value from intraday to multi-day horizons. Machine-learning, reinforcement-learning and evolutionary approaches that use these inputs are included. Open interest and liquidations belong to topic 04; on-chain flows to topic 19.

Coverage note: 21 sources were read. The session's web-search budget was exhausted for most of the run, so several known sub-literatures could not be reached (listed under "Where it disagrees or is weak" and in the reading-order section). Nothing below should be read as "no evidence exists" where it says "not found".

## What the literature agrees on

1. **Attention predicts activity and volatility more reliably than direction.** Tweet volume leads next-day realized volatility and trading volume for Bitcoin, with bilateral causality, and does not robustly lead returns ([Shen et al. 2019](notes/shen-2019-does-twitter-predict-bitcoin.md)). Where attention does predict returns it does so at one to two weeks and with R-squared of 1% to 2% ([Liu and Tsyvinski 2018](notes/liu-2018-risks-returns-cryptocurrency-attention.md)).

2. **In crypto, attention and retail participation are trend-confirming and lagging.** Search interest and price feed each other ([Kristoufek 2013](notes/kristoufek-2013-bitcoin-google-trends-wikipedia.md)); attention rises after returns ([Liu and Tsyvinski 2018](notes/liu-2018-risks-returns-cryptocurrency-attention.md)); new retail users arrive after price rises while large holders sell ([Auer et al. 2022](notes/auer-2022-crypto-trading-bitcoin-prices-retail.md)); retail brokerage clients who trade against moves in stocks and gold do not do so in crypto ([Kogan et al. 2023](notes/kogan-2023-are-cryptos-different-retail.md)); manual front-end traders on Hyperliquid open positions in the direction of recent returns while algorithmic participants open against them ([Jia et al. 2026](notes/jia-2026-who-loses-why-perpetual-futures-hyperliquid.md)).

3. **At intraday horizons, social sentiment mostly follows price.** With hourly data across nine coins, price returns are the strongest Granger-causal variable and sentiment is mostly an effect ([Kraaijeveld and De Smedt 2020](notes/kraaijeveld-2020-predictive-power-twitter-sentiment-crypto.md)). The BitMEX chat-room study in topic 04 reaches the same conclusion from a different angle: chat mood tracks the last hour or so of price action and the multi-week trend ([Soska et al. 2021](../04-open-interest-liquidations/notes/soska-2021-cryptocurrency-derivatives-bitmex-case-study.md)).

4. **Retail derivatives traders lose, mainly through costs and forced exits.** On Hyperliquid, front-end traders lost USD 1.11 billion over fifteen months, of which USD 725 million was fees and USD 226 million net liquidation losses ([Jia et al. 2026](notes/jia-2026-who-loses-why-perpetual-futures-hyperliquid.md)). On CME, the median small retail account loses a few hundred dollars and holds for days ([Ferko et al. 2024](notes/ferko-2024-retail-traders-futures-markets.md)). The loss is not primarily adverse selection, which weakens the simple "fade retail direction" story.

5. **Text adds a little to price features and nothing on its own.** A tweets-only model is the worst performer; fusion with price features improves recall for large up-moves but not down-moves, without statistical significance ([Zou and Herremans 2022](notes/zou-2022-prebit-multimodal-twitter-finbert.md)). In an LLM agent, removing news cut return in one ablation window, but the full agent still trailed buy-and-hold on SOL ([Li et al. 2024](notes/li-2024-cryptotrade-reflective-llm-agent.md)).

6. **LLM-scored news works in equities out of sample, and the edge is decaying.** Sharpe fell from 6.54 in 2021Q4 to 1.22 in early 2024 and the strategy dies at 20 bps round-trip ([Lopez-Lira and Tang 2023](notes/lopezlira-2023-chatgpt-forecast-stock-price.md)). Any LLM backtest inside the model's training window is contaminated ([Glasserman and Lin 2023](notes/glasserman-2023-lookahead-bias-gpt-sentiment.md)).

7. **Positioning labels describe accounts, not intentions.** The CFTC classifies traders, not trades ([CFTC TFF notes](notes/cftc-2010-traders-financial-futures-explanatory-notes.md)); Binance's ratios count accounts or top-20% positions and cannot see hedges ([Binance API definitions](notes/binance-2026-long-short-ratio-api-definitions.md)); a wallet's off-venue exposure is invisible on Hyperliquid ([Hyperliquid info endpoint](notes/hyperliquid-2026-info-endpoint-public-positions.md)).

## Where it disagrees or is weak

- **Is retail contrarian or momentum?** Contrarian in US equities with attention-driven buying of both winners and losers ([Barber and Odean 2008](notes/barber-2008-all-that-glitters-attention-buying.md)); contrarian in US micro futures, and as often short as long ([Ferko et al. 2024](notes/ferko-2024-retail-traders-futures-markets.md)); momentum-like in crypto spot-style accounts ([Kogan et al. 2023](notes/kogan-2023-are-cryptos-different-retail.md)) and on Hyperliquid ([Jia et al. 2026](notes/jia-2026-who-loses-why-perpetual-futures-hyperliquid.md)). Kalshi is a regulated US futures venue trading a crypto asset, so it sits between the two regimes and the sign is unknown.

- **Does sentiment predict returns at all?** Positive daily results exist for Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash and Litecoin over two months ([Kraaijeveld and De Smedt 2020](notes/kraaijeveld-2020-predictive-power-twitter-sentiment-crypto.md)) and for a polarization-based strategy over one year ([Garcia and Schweitzer 2015](notes/garcia-2015-social-signals-algorithmic-trading-bitcoin.md)). The longer four-year study concludes that tweet volume drives volatility and volume "but not returns", with only a weak and mixed return result ([Shen et al. 2019](notes/shen-2019-does-twitter-predict-bitcoin.md)). Samples are short, bubble-dominated, and pre-date the current structure of X.

- **Exchange long/short ratios have no rigorous test in what was found.** Two targeted searches returned only exchange posts, news and vendor blogs. The one documented episode shows the Binance top-trader account ratio moving against price at both extremes, with hedging as an alternative explanation ([Samsoedin and Wu 2025](notes/samsoedin-2025-binance-top-trader-ratio-contrarian.md)). The contrarian reading of the retail ratio and the smart-money reading of the top-trader ratio are folklore until tested.

- **Fear and greed indices.** Only the provider's definition was read ([Alternative.me](notes/alternativeme-2026-crypto-fear-greed-index.md)). Half of the index is price-derived. Academic tests were not retrieved.

- **COT positioning in crypto futures.** Only the regulator's definitions were read. No test of TFF categories against Bitcoin or Ether futures returns was located. The carry interpretation of leveraged-fund shorts is in topic 03 ([Schmeling et al. 2023](../03-perp-pricing-funding/notes/schmeling-2023-crypto-carry.md)).

- **Reinforcement learning with sentiment inputs is methodologically weak.** The one crypto example read reports a 30-day test and mixes its best return and best Sharpe from different settings ([Sattarov and Choi 2024](notes/sattarov-2024-multi-level-dqn-bitcoin-sentiment.md)). The general overfitting critique is in topic 16 ([Gort et al. 2022](../16-oscillators-technical-rules/notes/gort-2022-deep-rl-crypto-backtest-overfitting.md)).

- **Evolutionary methods on sentiment or positioning inputs: almost nothing.** Only a survey that places sentiment analysis and evolutionary portfolio optimization side by side was found ([Zamuda et al. 2019](notes/zamuda-2019-forecasting-crypto-sentiment-evolutionary-survey.md)). Genetic-programming work on price and order-book rules sits in topics [07](../07-order-book-imbalance/notes/funie-2014-genetic-programming-swarm-hft-rules.md), [08](../08-trade-flow-toxicity/notes/manahov-2021-efficiency-bitcoin-genetic-programming.md), [13](../13-momentum-reversal/notes/neely-1997-genetic-programming-technical-rules-fx.md) and [16](../16-oscillators-technical-rules/notes/allen-1999-genetic-algorithms-technical-trading-rules.md); none use sentiment or positioning features.

- **LLM crypto evidence is thin and daily.** One agent paper with three two-month windows ([Li et al. 2024](notes/li-2024-cryptotrade-reflective-llm-agent.md)). No intraday study of how fast crypto perps absorb scored headlines was found. Topic 02 has an LLM-scored monetary-policy note ([Nicolas 2026](../02-crypto-macro-linkages/notes/nicolas-2026-bitcoin-monetary-policy-expectations-llm.md)).

- **Data quality.** Bots are an estimated 1% to 14% of crypto tweets by a conservative heuristic ([Kraaijeveld and De Smedt 2020](notes/kraaijeveld-2020-predictive-power-twitter-sentiment-crypto.md)). Posted sentiment depends on the poster's position ([Zamuda et al. 2019](notes/zamuda-2019-forecasting-crypto-sentiment-evolutionary-survey.md)). Binance ratio history is limited to 30 days through the API.

## Open questions worth testing on SOL perps

1. **Lead or lag of Binance ratios.** At 5-minute to 4-hour horizons, do changes in the SOLUSDT global account ratio, top-trader account ratio and top-trader position ratio lead SOL returns, or only follow them? Run bilateral Granger tests and event studies on ratio extremes, conditioned on open-interest change (topic 04) and funding (topic 03). Start collecting now; the API keeps 30 days.
2. **Does the retail ratio rise after any large move?** The Barber-Odean prediction is that attention shocks raise retail buying regardless of sign. Test on the global account ratio after top-5% absolute hourly SOL returns.
3. **Front-end opening flow on Hyperliquid.** Reconstruct the Jia et al. classification for SOL and test whether net front-end opening flow predicts reversal over 15 to 120 minutes after costs, and whether the gap between front-end and algorithmic opening flow does better.
4. **Cohort positioning.** Do profitable-wallet and unprofitable-wallet net SOL exposures on Hyperliquid diverge before moves? How stable is "profitable" out of sample?
5. **Sign of retail flow on Kalshi.** Once Kalshi SOL-perp trade data accumulate, is small-lot flow contrarian (CME pattern) or momentum (Hyperliquid pattern) relative to the preceding hour's return?
6. **Hourly attention as a volatility input.** Does SOL-related post volume improve a 1-hour realized-volatility forecast beyond lagged volatility and volume (handing off to topic 14)?
7. **Trend-conditioned attention intraday.** Does the Kristoufek interaction (attention growth times trend sign) add to a pure momentum feature at 1 to 6 hours?
8. **Negative-attention ratio for SOL.** Build a ratio of outage, exploit and hack mentions to total SOL mentions and test it as a short-horizon bearish flag.
9. **Headline absorption speed.** With tick timestamps, how many seconds does SOL take to absorb an LLM-scored headline on Binance, and is any drift left after 1, 5 and 30 minutes? Use only models whose cut-off precedes the test window, and compare masked versus unmasked asset names.
10. **Fear-and-greed residual.** After regressing out the bot's own volatility and momentum features, does the index residual condition the performance of reversal or momentum signals?
11. **Search methods with guardrails.** If RL or evolutionary search is used to combine these features, require walk-forward splits, multiple seeds, a cost model with funding, and a buy-and-hold plus simple-trend benchmark; the sources read here fail most of these.
